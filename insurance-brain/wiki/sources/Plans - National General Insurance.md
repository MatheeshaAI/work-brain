# Plans - National General Insurance

Per-insurer file in the plan library ([[Plan library]]); entity page [[National General Insurance]]. Network: [[HealthNet]].

| Type | Plans | Price AED (excl. VAT) |
|---|---|---|
| Entry Level | Classic 4R, 4, 3R, 3 Enhanced | 3,392 - 5,062 |
| Good | Classic 2R, 2, 1R, 1 Enhanced | 5,832 - 8,035 |
| Best | Prime 2, Prime 1 Enhanced | 9,541 - 11,386 |

Co-pay: 20 percent up to AED 100 on consultation (twice the usual cap), 0 percent diagnostics and medicines, dental "enhanced". The "R" suffix variants are about AED 330-640 cheaper than the same-number plan (R likely means a restricted network; not stated). Entry-level here is not cheap: the entry plans start 2 to 3 times above Nextcare or Ecare entry plans.
