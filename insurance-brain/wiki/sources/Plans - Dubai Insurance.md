## Reading it
- The Dubai Care product line appears twice: the "Next" family (N5 from AED 1,450, N3, N2, N1) is about 30-35 percent cheaper than the original family at the N5 entry level but within a few percent of it at N3 to N1. Suffix letters A, B, C vary price by a few hundred dirhams.
- The N-number follows the Dubai Care ladder in [[Network tiers]] (N5 lowest, N1 highest).
- Co-pay: 20 percent max AED 50 on consultation, 20 percent on diagnostics, medicines or pharmacy; only Exclusive N2 has 0 percent diagnostics and pharmacy.
- Cheapest entry options sit on Dubai Care Next N5 (AED 1,561.35 with VAT) and Ecare Blue (1,605.45).

## Flags
- Dubai Care N1 (AED 7,317) is cheaper than Exclusive N2 (7,416), although the network ladder puts N1 above N2.
- "Silver Classic C" has an empty co-pay cell.
- The 2026-05-22 extraction plan names the entry-level plan "Remento N4"; this file shows it as "Dubai Care N4" on the "Remnto" network label, confirming the spelling confusion ([[Entry level plans]]).
