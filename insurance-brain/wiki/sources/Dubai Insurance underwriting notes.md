# Source: Dubai Insurance underwriting notes

`raw/Health-Insurance/Workspace/Knowledge Base/Dubai-Insurance.md`, headed "Dubai Insurance - Underwriting Notes", recorded **April 2026**.

## The rule: child-alone policies on the Ramento plans
**Applicable age range: 6 - 12 years.** A child can be covered alone only if **one** of these conditions is met:

1. The sponsor parent is insured with **any insurer** - a copy of that policy has to be uploaded.
2. Both parents are insured with **any insurer** - policy copy uploaded.
3. One parent is insured with **Dubai Insurance on the same or a higher plan** - policy copy uploaded.

So there are two ways in: a parent holds cover anywhere, or the parent holds cover with this insurer at an equal or higher tier. In every branch the evidence is the same - the sponsor's own policy copy.

## How it is used
This is an eligibility gate, not a pricing rule: it decides whether a quote can be issued for a child on their own, and it tells the desk what to ask the client for up front. It pairs with [[General underwriting rules]], which sets the medical-evidence rules, and with [[Documentation requirements]].

## Caveats
- The note is **plan-family specific** ("Ramento plans") and says nothing about the other 63 Dubai Insurance plans in the library, or about whether the rule extends to them. Do not generalise it beyond the family without asking the insurer.
- It is a **claim by the desk's note-taker**, not a quotation from the insurer's own wording - there is no source document attached to it. Compare the naming caveat in [[Dubai Insurance]]: the same sheet spells the family "Remento", "Remnto" and "RN4".
- It is dated April 2026, six weeks before the plan library snapshot.
