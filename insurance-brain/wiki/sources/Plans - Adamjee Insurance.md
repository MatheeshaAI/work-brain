# Plans - Adamjee Insurance

Per-insurer file in the plan library ([[Plan library]]); entity page [[Adamjee]].

## Mednet (14 plans)
Silk Road (Entry), Pearl, Emerald, Green, Silver Classic (Good), Silver Premium, Gold (Best). Each appears twice: a plain version and a "750" version priced about AED 2-10 lower, with a blank co-pay cell. Prices excl. VAT run 2,727.41 (Silk Road 750) to 7,925.83 (Gold). Standard co-pay: 20 percent max AED 50 on consultation, 20 percent on diagnostics and medicines. Mednet names follow the tier ladder in [[Network tiers]] (Silk Road lowest, Gold highest).

## NAS (5 plans)
"FCP" plans WN (Entry, 2,685.56), SRN, RN (Good, 3,695.50 and 4,815.12), GN, CN (Best, 8,181.55 and 9,300.54). Co-pay is a flat AED 50 on consultation, 0 percent diagnostics, 20 percent medicines. WN to CN follows the NAS ladder.

## Flags
- The meaning of "750" and "FCP" is not explained (a deductible or limit variant is likely). Unconfirmed.
- Insurer entity [[Adamjee]] previously appeared only in the May audits; this file shows it has a full plan range on two networks.
