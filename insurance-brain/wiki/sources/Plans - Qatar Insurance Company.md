# Plans - Qatar Insurance Company

Per-insurer file in the plan library ([[Plan library]]); entity page [[Qatar Insurance Company]].

## QIC own network (12 plans, all Entry Level)
Three families of four: BLUE 01-04 (AED 1,186 / 1,276 / 1,328 / 1,484), GREEN 1-4 (1,672 / 1,763 / 1,852 / 2,057), CLASSIC 1-4 (2,066 / 2,187 / 2,281 / 2,550). Within a family the 1 has 20 percent diagnostics and medicines, the 2 and 3 have 10 percent, and the 4 has 0 percent. Consultation: 20 percent up to **AED 25** (half the usual cap of 50). Blue 01 at 1,186 is the second-cheapest entry plan in the library after Takaful Emarat APN and Fidelity Plan G.

## QIC Mednet (7 plans)
PLAN7 Entry (2,433), PLAN6 to PLAN3 Good (2,801 / 3,304 / 3,789 / 4,942), PLAN2 and PLAN1_QIC Best (6,461 / 7,281). Standard 20 percent max AED 50 co-pay.

## Flags
- Quote-specific pricing: the same Blue plans cost AED 4,204 to 4,701 in [[Filtered Plans 4000-6000 AED]] (a larger quote), so a library price is only a guide ([[Plan library]]).
- QIC's own network is not named separately from the insurer, so the facility list is not in these files; the entity [[Qatar Insurance Company]] was a stub in the May audits and now has a plan range.
