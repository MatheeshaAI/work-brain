# Plans - Abu Dhabi National Takaful

Per-insurer file in the plan library ([[Plan library]]); entity page [[Abu Dhabi National Takaful Insurance]]. All plans use the [[NAS]] network.

## Structure
| Type | Plans | Price AED (excl. VAT) | Names |
|---|---|---|---|
| Entry Level | 5 | 2,228 - 2,649 | Core, Care WN, WN-a, WN-b, WN-c |
| Good | 11 | 3,915 - 5,655 | Care SRN (a-c), Care RN (a-d), Dubai 2, Dubai 3 |
| Best | 14 | 7,805 - 29,273 | Care GN (a-d), Care CN (a-d), Dubai 1, IPMI 1, IPMI 2 (two geographies) |

## Reading the names
The suffixes WN, SRN, RN, GN and CN follow the NAS network ladder (Workers, Super-Restricted, Restricted, General, Comprehensive), so price rises with the NAS tier; letters a, b, c, d vary the co-pay. See [[Network tiers]]. Co-pay options include "20 percent max AED 15 for GP and 25 for specialist", "10 percent max AED 25", and up to "10 percent max AED 35 GP / 20 percent max AED 75 specialist".

## Cover beyond the UAE
IPMI 1 and IPMI 2 (worldwide excluding the US) cost AED 20,579 and 21,392; IPMI 2 worldwide AED 29,273 (30,775.50 with VAT) is the most expensive plan in this file.

## Observations
- "Dubai 1, 2, 3" are plan names, not the Dubai Care network; do not confuse them with [[Dubai Care]].
- Several co-pay texts are identical across different prices, so the benefit difference between options is in the schedule of benefits, not in this file.
