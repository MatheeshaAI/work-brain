## Dubai Insurance - Ramento child-alone policies
Ages 6 to 12 only. A child-alone policy is accepted only if one of: the sponsor parent is insured with any insurer; both parents are insured with any insurer; one parent is insured with [[Dubai Insurance]] on the same or a higher plan. A policy copy must be uploaded in each case.

## Takaful Emarat - bank transfer delay
Bank transfers take one business day to reflect; documents are issued only after confirmation, so a transfer client gets the policy about 24 hours later, never the same day. Set that expectation up front. See [[Takaful Emarat]].

## General - age over 65
- Applies above 65 (unclear for exactly 65, so apply when over 65 to be safe).
- Insurers need a completed medical report / pre-underwriting form before offering terms: member details; medical history (diabetes, hypertension, cardiovascular, respiratory, renal, cancer, neurological or psychiatric, other chronic); past medical and surgical history; current medications; physical exam (height, weight, BMI, blood pressure, pulse); treatment plan.
- Eight tests attached, sourced by the member: CBC, fasting blood sugar, lipid profile, kidney function, liver function, ECG stress test, abdomen and pelvis ultrasound, PSA (males only).
- Pre-existing conditions: declaration is optional but needs reports and current prescriptions; if undisclosed, claims may be rejected.
- All reports must come from a UAE facility and be dated within the last 3 months.
