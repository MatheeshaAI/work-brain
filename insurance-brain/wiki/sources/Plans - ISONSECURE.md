# Plans - ISONSECURE

Per-insurer file in the plan library ([[Plan library]]); entity page [[ISONSECURE Insurance]].

| Type | Plans | Price AED (excl. VAT) |
|---|---|---|
| Good | Classic Bronze Dental 4 and 3, Classic Silver Dental 4 and 3 | 5,590.30 - 7,267.28 |
| Best | Classic Gold Dental 4 and 3, Platinum | 9,654.77 - 16,163.38 |

Co-pay: 20 percent max AED 50 on consultation, 0 percent diagnostics and medicines, 20 percent dental and 10 percent optical on the Classic range; Platinum drops the dental and optical co-pay lines. "Dental 3" costs about AED 325 more than "Dental 4" at every level. The network and the insurer share one name in the CRM, so the network column cannot be used to tell them apart ([[Network tiers]]).
