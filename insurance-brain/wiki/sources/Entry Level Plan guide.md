# Entry Level Plan guide

The file the earlier concept page [[Entry level plans]] said might define the set differently. It does: here entry level is **a price band across eight networks**, not the four named plans of the extraction plan.

## Structure
A section per network, each with a "best for" line, a table of plan, provider, price, price with VAT and key co-pay features, and a network note: Nextcare (9 plans from Fidelity United G/F/E up to HAYAH, Takaful Emarat Silver, Orient RN3, RAK RN3), E Care Blue (7: Takaful Emarat Blue 1-4 and Dubai Insurance Blue 01-03), Dubai Care, Qatar Insurance Company, NAS, Mednet, Aafiya, and other networks. It ends with comparison blocks (most affordable, best value, premium entry level) and selection tips (budget-conscious, better coverage, large families, citizens and residents).

## Headline figures (as stated)
Total entry-level plans 80+; networks covered 8; price range AED 1,163 to 3,519.

## Flags
- **Floor mismatch:** the stated minimum is 1,163 (Fidelity Plan G) but Takaful Emarat APN is AED 1,114 in the same data, and APN appears in the Aafiya section ([[Plans - Takaful Emarat Insurance]]).
- **Count mismatch:** 80+ against 77 Entry Level rows in [[Plans Table 418 Plans]].
- **Fidelity included here** but excluded by the basic plan filter and the plan-download skill ([[Basic plan filter]]); another disagreement between the filters and this guide.
- Network descriptions ("government backing", "strong presence in major emirates") are the guide's own wording, not sourced.
- Three definitions of entry level already existed ([[Entry level plans]]); this is a fourth: a price band by network ([[CRM plan type labels]]).
