# Plans - Abu Dhabi National Insurance Company

One of the 22 per-insurer files in the plan library ([[Plan library]]); entity page [[Abu Dhabi National Insurance Company]]. Prices are from a single lead's quote scrape ("last updated 2026-05-01") and change with the members.

## Structure
All 36 plans sit on one network, the insurer's own network, grouped by plan type ([[CRM plan type labels]]):
| Type | Plans | Price (AED, VAT excluded) | Families |
|---|---|---|---|
| Entry Level | 4 | 3,178 - 3,935 | Shifa Bronze 1-4 |
| Good | 7 | 5,282 - 7,405 | Shifa Silver 5, 9, 13, 14 and Silver with Dental options 2, 6, 10 |
| Best | 25 | 6,516 - 14,966 | Shifa Silver 1; Shifa Gold with optional dental and optical add-ons (options 1 to 24) |

## Co-pay pattern
Consultation 20 percent capped at AED 30 or AED 50 (the two numbers distinguish option families), diagnostics 0 percent, medicines 0, 10 or 20 percent; dental plans add 20 percent on routine dental and 50 percent on major; optical 0 percent. Prices with VAT run AED 3,428.25 to 15,805.65. See [[Co-pay and co-insurance]].

## Observations
- Cheapest in the file is a Bronze with 20 percent medicines; the cheaper the plan, the higher the medicine share.
- Dental and optical add-ons add roughly AED 1,100-2,500 to a Gold base.
- Flag: Shifa Silver 1 (AED 6,516) is labelled Best while Shifa Silver 9 (6,448) and Silver Dental 10 (7,336) are Good, so the label is not purely by tier name.
