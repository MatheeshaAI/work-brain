# Source: UAE Health Insurance Plan Structure and Pricing

Two documents. `health_insurance_plan_options.md` is the mind-map and decision tree (structure only). `Plans.md` is the same structure plus the **actual pricing data** copied from the CRM catalogue. The structure is summarised in [[Plan tiers and variables]]; this page records the priced tables and the cross-source differences.

## Structure (both documents)
| Tier | Name | Coverage | Price level |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Basic | clinic visits only (outpatient) | lowest |
| 2 | Basic Enhanced | extended clinics plus specialist access | low to mid |
| 3 | Enhanced, Levels 1 to 6 | hospital cover, outpatient and inpatient, with level = number and quality of hospitals (1 entry, 2 more, 3 premium, 4 top-tier, 5 and 6 maximum) | mid to high |
| 4 | International | worldwide | highest |

Each plan then varies by TPA (Nextcare, MEDNET, eCare, Aafiya, NAS, DubaiCare, ADNIC, Sukoon) and co-pay (0%, 10%, 20%): "100+ variations". A decision tree asks clinic only, hospital in the UAE, or international, then TPA and co-pay.

## Co-pay impact
0% = highest premium, nothing at the visit; 10% medium; 20% lowest premium, 20% of the bill at the visit. See [[Co-pay and co-insurance]].

## Customer to tier map
| Customer | Tier | Key question |
|---|---|---|
| young single professional | Basic / Basic Enhanced | "Just need clinic visits?" |
| young couple, no kids | Basic Enhanced / Enhanced L1 to L2 | "Planning kids soon?" |
| family with young kids | Enhanced L3 to L4 | "Which hospital for emergencies?" |
| family with teens | Enhanced L4 to L6 | "Any specialist needs?" |
| child only | Basic Enhanced / Enhanced | "Pediatric network important?" |
| older expat (40+) | Enhanced L4 to L6 | "Pre-existing conditions?" |
| frequent traveller | International | "Need cover outside the UAE?" |
| self-employed or budget | Basic / Basic Enhanced | "Budget priority?" |

## Priced tables in Plans.md (AED, per person, catalogue snapshot)
- **Basic (clinic only), 32 plans:** from about 1,163 (a NEXTCARE-network Fidelity United plan; 1,260 with VAT) up to about 2,314 (an eCare "Classic Care" plan; 2,468.55 with VAT). Insurers: Fidelity United, Qatar Insurance (Blue, Green, Classic series), [[Takaful Emarat]] (E Care Blue, APN, Essential, Silver), [[Dubai Insurance]] (Dubai Care Next N5 variants, Blue, Classic Care). Typical consultation co-pay 20% capped at AED 25 or 50; diagnostics 0 to 20%; medicines 0 to 30%. VAT shown as price times 1.05.
- **Basic Enhanced, 27 plans:** from about 1,635 (Hayah Care Protect RN3 B, Nextcare) to about 3,302 (Watania Takaful Silk Road, MEDNET, 20% up to AED 75). Networks: Nextcare RN3, NAS, MEDNET Silk Road, Orient Takaful, QIC-MEDNET. Level label mostly "Entry Level".
- **Enhanced, 19 plans:** from about 1,499 (Takaful Emarat Essential, Aafiya) to about 4,164 (Takaful Emarat Opal, NAS, GP 25 / specialist 60). Mostly "Good" level; MEDNET Pearl across Takaful Emarat, Dubai Insurance, Adamjee, NLGIC, Orient, Watania; plus Dubai Care N4, Aafiya APN+, Hayah RN2, Iridium.

## Cross-source differences (flag)
- [[Plan price tiers]] gives different "starting prices" (for example Takaful Emarat Essential 2,417.10 as the Enhanced starting point versus 1,499.40 here). The two are different snapshots, the first from a June 2026 report, this one from the early-2026 catalogue; plan prices and "Hidden / Currently Online" flags move.
- Some entries appear in two tables (Takaful Emarat Essential in both Basic and Enhanced lists, at different prices) so tier labels are not stable between snapshots; compare [[Insurance plan catalogue]].
- [[Health Insurance Expert System Prompt]] uses a different tier vocabulary (Basic, Silver, Gold, Platinum).
- Table rows are catalogue data, not client data; no personal information.
