# Plans - Daman

Per-insurer file in the plan library ([[Plan library]]); entity page [[Daman Insurance]] (National Health Insurance Company - Daman). Network: Daman's own.

| Type | Plans | Price AED (excl. VAT) |
|---|---|---|
| Entry Level | Care Bronze DNE DM5; Care Bronze DNE with Dental | 10,392 - 10,464 |
| Good | Care Silver DNE DM5; with Dental | 12,335 - 12,407 |
| Best | Care Gold (2), Care Platinum (2), Premier DNE | 15,916 - 45,506 |

Co-pay: 20 percent up to AED 50 on consultation with 10 percent diagnostics and medicines (Bronze, Silver), Gold 0 percent diagnostics, Platinum 0 on all three, Premier NIL on consultation. The "with dental" variants add only about AED 72-830.

Flag: the Entry Level label here sits above AED 10,000, versus AED 1,114-2,000 for entry plans elsewhere ([[Plan price tiers]]), so "Entry Level" is a position within the insurer's own range, not a price band ([[CRM plan type labels]]).
