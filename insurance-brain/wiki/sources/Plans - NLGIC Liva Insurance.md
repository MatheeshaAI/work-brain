# Plans - NLGIC Liva Insurance

Per-insurer file in the plan library ([[Plan library]]); entity page [[NLGIC Liva]].

| Network | Entry | Good | Best |
|---|---|---|---|

Prices AED excluding VAT. Co-pay is the standard 20 percent max AED 50 on consultation, 20 percent diagnostics and medicines on all 12.

Observations: NLGIC Liva's Mednet plans are priced about 5-10 percent below Orient Insurance's and Adamjee's for the same sub-network names, e.g. Silk Road 2,521 against 2,824 and 2,727 ([[Plans - Orient Insurance]], [[Plans - Adamjee Insurance]]). Its NAS Plan 5 at AED 2,036 is the cheapest NAS plan in the library.
