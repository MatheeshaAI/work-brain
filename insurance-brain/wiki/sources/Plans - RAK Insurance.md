# Plans - RAK Insurance

Per-insurer file in the plan library ([[Plan library]]); entity page [[RAK Insurance]].

| Network | Plans (price AED excl. VAT) |
|---|---|
| [[Mednet]] (FCP) | Silk Road 2,671; Pearl 2,855 (Entry); Emerald 3,364; Green 4,167; Silver Classic 5,012; Gold 7,753 (Good); Silver Premium 6,962 (Best) |
| [[Nextcare]] | RN3 1,617 (Entry); RN2 4,001, RN 4,389 (Good); GN Limited 6,110, GN 7,261, GN+ 8,067 (Best) |
| RAK NAS | Workers OP 2,505, SRN 3,437 (Entry); RN 4,479 (Good); GN 7,613, CN 8,626 (Best) |

Co-pay wording differs from other insurers: "Deductible - 20 percent up to max 75", with 0 percent on outpatient pharmacy for the lower Mednet plans and 20 percent on diagnosis and pharmacy for the rest, in place of the usual 20 percent up to AED 50. Nextcare rows use the standard line.

Flag: Gold (7,753) is labelled Good while Silver Premium (6,962) is Best, an inversion of the Mednet ladder in [[Network tiers]]. The Nextcare prices nearly match [[Plans - Orient Insurance]] and [[Plans - HAYAH Insurance]].
