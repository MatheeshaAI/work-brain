# Plans - DIC-Viventa

Per-insurer file in the plan library ([[Plan library]]); entity page [[DIC-Viventa]].

| Type | Plans | Price AED (excl. VAT) |
|---|---|---|
| Good | 7 | 3,180.57 (500 Silk Road) to 6,417.49 (1000 Silver Classic) |
| Best | 2 | 8,372.38 (1000 Silver Premium), 9,428.20 (1000 Gold) |

Plan names combine a number (500, 750, 1000) with a [[Mednet]] sub-network: Silk Road, Emerald, Green, Silver Classic, Silver Premium, Gold. Co-pay is the standard 20 percent max AED 50 on consultation, 20 percent diagnostics and medicines. The number likely stands for a deductible or annual limit variant (not stated).

Flag: "DIC" appears in the renewal files as shorthand for Dubai Insurance ([[Renewals Pipeline April 2026]]), but this file is titled DIC-Viventa and is separate from [[Plans - Dubai Insurance]]; whether Viventa is a product line of Dubai Insurance is not stated. Silk Road is classed Good here but Entry Level in other files.
