# Source: Health Insurance plan categoris

Original note: [[raw/10_Work-Insurance/Health Insurance plan categoris.md|Health Insurance plan categoris]]. Tables of Basic, Basic Enhance, and Enhance plans with copays and prices.

**Basic Plans:** Includes Fidelity United Plan G/F (NEXTCARE), Qatar Insurance BLUE series, Takaful Emarat E Care Blue/APN/Essential, Dubai Insurance Dubai Care Next N5/Blue series/ECARE, etc. Prices range ~1163-2468 AED.

**Basic Enhance Plans:** HAYAH CARE PROTECT RN3, Takaful Emarat Silver (NEXTCARE/MEDNET), Dubai Insurance Dubai Care Next N5A, Orient RN3, RAK RN3, NLGIC/ADNT/WATANIA NAS plans, Silk Road MEDNET plans. Prices ~1634-3302 AED.

**Enhance Plans:** Takaful Emarat Essential/APN+, Qatar Insurance PLAN6/PLAN5, MEDNET Pearl/Emerald, Dubai Insurance Pearl/Emerald/Dubai Care N4 variants. Prices ~1499-4164 AED.

Details include copays (consultation/diagnostics/medicines) and networks/TPAs.
