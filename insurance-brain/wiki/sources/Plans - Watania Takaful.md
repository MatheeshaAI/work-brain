# Plans - Watania Takaful

Per-insurer file in the plan library ([[Plan library]]); entity page [[Watania Takaful Insurance]].

| Network | Entry | Good | Best |
|---|---|---|---|
| [[Mednet]] | Silk Road 3,143 | Emerald 3,957, Green 4,909 | Silver Classic 5,917, Silver Premium 8,200, Gold 9,162 |
| [[NAS]] | Plan 6 and Plan 5, both 2,568 | Plan 4 4,114, Plan 3 5,086 | Plan 2 8,115, Plan 1 10,747 |
Prices AED excluding VAT.

Co-pay differs from the market norm: Mednet plans are 20 percent up to AED 75 on consultation with only 10 percent on diagnostics and medicines; NAS plans are 10 percent (GP, capped AED 15 to 35) and 20 percent (specialist, capped AED 25 to 75) with 10 percent diagnostics and 15 percent medicines.

Flags: Silver Classic (AED 5,917) is Best here but Good at most other insurers; Plan 5 and Plan 6 have identical prices and co-pay. Watania's Mednet range costs 15-25 percent more than NLGIC Liva or Orient for the same sub-network names, in exchange for the lighter co-pay.
