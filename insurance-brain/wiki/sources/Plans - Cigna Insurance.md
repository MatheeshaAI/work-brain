# Plans - Cigna Insurance

Per-insurer file in the plan library ([[Plan library]]); entity page [[Cigna Insurance]]. Every plan is on the [[Neuron]] network and all are classed Best; there is no entry-level product.

## Structure
Product families: **Regional** (UAE and region), **International**, **International Plus**, each with levels **General**, **General Plus**, **Comprehensive** (and "Comprehensive excl. AH", meaning excluding an add-on), optional **Healthy Connect** and a **WW** (worldwide) or **WWE** (worldwide excluding the US) reach. Names are formulas like "International Plus - Comprehensive + Healthy Connect WW".

## Price shape
| Family | Cheapest | Dearest (excl. VAT) |
|---|---|---|
| Regional | 12,433.69 (General) | 23,036.26 |
| International | 13,321.81 (General) | 37,760.36 |
| International Plus | 14,802.00 (General) | 45,576.23 |
VAT-inclusive range AED 13,094.22 to 47,893.89. Adding Healthy Connect and worldwide reach each lifts price; WW adds more than WWE.

## Co-pay
One line for all 40: 20 percent up to AED 100 per visit. No diagnostics or medicines split.

## Use
These are for clients asking for international cover (the "Enhanced" tier's international option in [[Plan price tiers]]); they are far outside the entry-level band.
