# Plans - Orient Insurance

Per-insurer file in the plan library ([[Insurance plan catalogue]]); entity page [[Orient Insurance]]. **Correction (2026-10-22):** this page previously said Orient is written `OI2` in renewal files. It is not - `OI2` is [[Oman Insurance]]. Orient has no renewal book; it appears only in the commission exports under its own name.

| Network | Entry | Good | Best |
|---|---|---|---|
| [[Mednet]] | Silk Road 2,824 | Pearl 3,018, Emerald 3,556, Green 4,410, Silver Classic 5,300 | Silver Premium 7,360, Gold 8,199 |
| [[Nextcare]] | RN3 Plan 1,559 | RN2 3,997, RN 4,384, GN Limited 6,106 | GN 7,257, GN Plus 8,063 |
Prices AED excluding VAT. Co-pay for all 13: 20 percent max AED 50 on consultation, 20 percent diagnostics and medicines.

Observations: the Nextcare prices are within about 1 percent of [[RAK Insurance]]'s (RN3 1,617, GN 7,261) and track [[HAYAH Insurance]], suggesting the TPA network drives the premium more than the insurer on this network. Flag: GN Limited (6,106) is classed Good while GN (7,257) is Best, and the GN Plus is Best, consistent with the ladder in [[Network tiers]].
