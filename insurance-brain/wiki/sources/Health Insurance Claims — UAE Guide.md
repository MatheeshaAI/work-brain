# Source: Health Insurance Claims — What to Cover in Your Content

Original note: [[raw/10_Work-Insurance/09-claims-guide-health|09-claims-guide-health]]. Reference for accurate claims content.

## Claim types
1. **Cashless (direct billing)** - Insurer pays hospital directly; network hospitals only; needs pre-authorization except emergencies (most common in UAE premium).
2. **Reimbursement** - Pay hospital, claim back; works at any hospital (higher % in-network); longer (7-21 days), full docs.
3. **Pre-authorization** - Required for planned admissions/surgeries/high-cost procedures; hospital submits before admission; approval 2-48 hours; without it cashless won't work unless emergency.
4. **Emergency claims** - Nearest hospital; call insurer within 24-48 hours; retrospective pre-auth; out-of-network may mean reimbursement.

## Common rejections
- **Pre-auth not obtained** - planned admission without calling insurer first
- **Non-network hospital** - chose out of network
- **Missing documentation** - no discharge summary/bills/prescriptions
- **Treatment not covered** - exclusions (cosmetic, experimental, some dental)
- **Sublimits exceeded** - room rent/ICU/doctor fees caps
- **Waiting period not completed** - pre-existing/maternity within waiting period

## Processes
**Planned admission (cashless):** Doctor recommends → hospital checks insurance → pre-auth submitted → approved → admission with card → treatment → bills insurer directly → sign at discharge (pay exclusions).
**Emergency:** Go to hospital/call ambulance → call insurer within 24-48h → cashless if in-network else may reimburse → submit docs within 14 days.
**Reimbursement:** Pay in full → collect original bill, discharge summary, reports, prescriptions, card copy → submit within 14-30 days → 7-21 days to credit.
