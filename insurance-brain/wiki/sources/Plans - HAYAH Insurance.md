# Plans - HAYAH Insurance

Per-insurer file in the plan library ([[Plan library]]); entity page [[HAYAH Insurance]]. All 21 plans are "Care Protect" on [[Nextcare]], with A, B, C letter variants.

| Type | Plans | Price AED (excl. VAT) |
|---|---|---|
| Entry Level | 3 (RN3 B, RN3 A, RN3) | 1,520 - 1,641 |
| Good | 10 (RN2 A, RN2, RN, RN A/B/C, GN Lite and A/B/C) | 3,908 - 5,976 |
| Best | 8 (GN, GN A/B/C, GN Plus and A/B/C) | 7,039 - 7,878 |

Names match the Nextcare network ladder (RN3, RN2, RN, GN Limited/Lite, GN, GN Plus) in [[Network tiers]]; the CRM list shows the same network sold by [[Orient Insurance]] and [[RAK Insurance]] at almost the same price. Co-pay for every row: 20 percent max AED 50 on consultation, 20 percent diagnostics and medicines.

**Care Protect RN3 B at AED 1,520 (1,634.85 with VAT)** is the example "Basic Enhanced" plan in [[Plan price tiers]]; there it is quoted from AED 1,461.60 and from 1,291.50 in a chat export, so the figure differs by source. See also [[Plans - April Hayah]] for the separate Mednet range.
