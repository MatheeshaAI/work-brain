# Plans - Fidelity United

Per-insurer file in the plan library ([[Plan library]]); entity page [[Fidelity United]]. All plans are on [[Nextcare]].

| Type | Plans | Price AED (excl. VAT) | With VAT |
|---|---|---|---|
| Entry Level | G, F, E | 1,163 / 1,364 / 1,433 | 1,260 / 1,471.05 / 1,543.50 |
| Good | D, C | 3,631 / 3,979 | 3,851.40 / 4,216.80 |
| Best | B, A | 6,567 / 7,294 | 6,934.20 / 7,697.55 |

Co-pay for all seven: 20 percent up to AED 50 per consultation, 20 percent diagnostics, **30 percent on medicines** (the highest medicine share of any insurer in the library).

Flags
- Plan G at AED 1,163 is the cheapest entry-level plan in [[Entry Level Plan guide]] and [[Plans Table 418 Plans]] except Takaful Emarat's APN (AED 1,114).
- The plan-download skill and the extension's basic filter both exclude Fidelity United ("not Basic" / "per underwriting preference"), so the cheapest plan is deliberately left out of the standard basic comparison. The reason is not recorded. See [[Basic plan filter]], [[Health Insurance CRM system overview]].
