# Plans - Orient-Allianz

Per-insurer file in the plan library ([[Plan library]]); entity page [[Orient-Allianz]].

## Structure
Product family **Sphera** at four levels: Essence, Base, Enhanced, Signature, each in General, General Plus or Comprehensive form, combined with Dental 1, 2 or 3. 16 Sphera rows plus one "Dubai Prime Silver (Comprehensive) Dental 3" row at the top, AED 27,128.76 (28,524.05 with VAT), whose co-pay differs (20 percent max AED 100 per visit).

| Level | Cheapest (excl. VAT) |
|---|---|
| Essence RNE Dental 1 | 11,271.42 |
| Base General Dental 1 | 13,327.58 |
| Enhanced General Plus Dental 1 | 15,777.17 |
| Signature General Plus Dental 1 | 18,627.32 |
| Signature Comprehensive Dental 3 | 25,384.32 |

Co-pay: 20 percent on consultation, nil diagnostics, 30 percent medicines on Essence and Base; nil medicines on Enhanced and Signature. Dental level 3 adds roughly AED 2,000-2,500 over level 1.

Network label: Allianz (insurer's partner network). All plans are classed Best, so none fits the entry-level work in [[Entry level plans]].
