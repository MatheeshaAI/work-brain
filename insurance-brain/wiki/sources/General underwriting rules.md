# Source: General underwriting rules

`raw/Health-Insurance/Workspace/Knowledge Base/General-Underwriting.md`, headed "General Underwriting - Industry-Wide Rules", recorded **April 2026**. It applies to **all insurers**, and it is the file that decides when a quote needs medical evidence behind it.

## Age 65+ medical underwriting
**Applies to members above 65 years.** The note flags the boundary itself: the requirement for age exactly 65 is unclear, so the desk applies it above 65 to be safe.

When quoting or booking for a member above 65, insurers require a completed **Medical Report / Pre-Underwriting Form** before they can offer terms.

### The medical form fields
**Member details** - full name, date of birth, gender, nationality, passport / Emirates ID number.

**Medical history** - tick all that apply and give details: diabetes mellitus, hypertension, cardiovascular disease, respiratory disease, renal disease, cancer (any type), neurological / psychiatric disorders, other chronic illnesses.

**Past history** - medical and surgical. **Current medications.** **Physical examination** - height, weight, BMI, blood pressure, pulse, general examination findings. **Treatment plan.**

### Required laboratory and diagnostic tests
Eight tests, which the **member** has to source and attach:

1. CBC (Complete Blood Count)
2. Fasting Blood Sugar
3. Complete Lipid Profile
4. Kidney Function Tests
5. Liver Function Tests
6. ECG Stress Test
7. Ultrasound - abdomen and pelvis
8. Prostate Specific Antigen *(males only)*

### Pre-existing conditions - declaration
Declaration is **optional**, but the note is explicit about the trade-off: if a condition is declared, the member must provide the **relevant medical reports for each condition** and **current medication prescriptions**; if it is **not disclosed, claims may be rejected**.

### Document validity
> All medical reports and documents must:
> - be issued by a **UAE-based** medical facility
> - be dated **within the last 3 months**

Reports from outside the UAE, or older than three months, will not be accepted by underwriters.

## How the desk uses it
This is the file behind two behaviours visible elsewhere in the workspace:
- The lead dossier note that medical tests are "required only if no declared conditions AND the member is 65+ years old" - the same boundary and the same test list.
- The FTC email, which tells the client that all known or suspected conditions must be declared and that failure to disclose may affect coverage - see [[Policy delivery and review request]] and [[Customer objections]].

## Caveats
- **The 65 boundary is uncertain by the note's own admission** ("requirement for age 65 itself is unclear"). Applying it above 65 is a safe-side decision, not a stated insurer rule.
- **"Any insurer" is the note's phrasing for the testing requirement**, which is claimed to be industry-wide; there is no insurer citation in the file.
- It is dated **April 2026** and was not re-verified when the plan library was refreshed on 1 May 2026.
- The rule set is silent on the **child-alone** eligibility gates - those are insurer-specific, see [[Dubai Insurance underwriting notes]].
