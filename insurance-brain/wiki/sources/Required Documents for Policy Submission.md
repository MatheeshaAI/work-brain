# Source: Required Documents for Policy Submission

A two-copy checklist (the work_os copy adds the closing backlink and tag). Rules are as recorded by the owner; regulator rules can change, so verify with the insurer before relying on a figure. The concept summary is [[Policy submission documents]] and fines are in [[Late insurance fines]].

## Base set
Medical Application Form; member passport, visa and Emirates ID; **sponsor** passport, visa and Emirates ID (when the member is a dependent).

## Company-purchased policy (employee cover)
Trade licence, VAT certificate, establishment card.

## Newborns
Birth certificate; discharge summary (not required after one year); if the discharge summary is from the home country, a paediatrician's fitness certificate.

## General notes
Documents must be valid and current; originals may be requested; insurers can ask for more. Newborn documents are only for the first year. **Pre-existing conditions:** supporting medical reports, prescriptions and treatment records from the last 3 months in the UAE. **Members aged 75 or over:** a fitness certificate with test reports is required by all insurers.

## By visa
| Case | Required beyond the medical form |
|---|---|
| New member to the UAE (Dubai visa) | Emirates ID application form, e-visa, passport, sponsor documents if dependent |
| Northern Emirates visa | change of status and passport only; sponsor documents if dependent |
| Abu Dhabi visa | passport and visa, Emirates ID if a renewal, **Certificate of Continuity (COC) mandatory for renewals** |
| New entrant | change of status (or entry stamp if unavailable), passport, sponsor documents if applicable |

## Fines for late insurance (as stated)
- New entrants: AED 200 if not insured within 15 days; AED 300 after 30 days; AED 300 per month thereafter.
- Renewals: AED 300 if not insured within the 30-day grace period, repeating monthly.

## Caveats
- The two 'AED 300' lines overlap (new entrants "after 30 days" and "per month thereafter"), so the monthly fine start date is ambiguous in the note.
- The rules mix emirate-level regimes (Dubai, Northern Emirates, Abu Dhabi) and are presented flat; for Abu Dhabi the COC rule is specific.
- Matches the post-quote paperwork flow in [[Document request handling]]; the CRM stores 16 documents per issued policy ([[IMCRM health detail page extraction]]).
- The file backlinks a note called "Insurance Companies", which exists only as a list of insurers (see [[Health Insurance Companies]]).
