# Late insurance fines

As recorded in [[Required Documents for Policy Submission]] (verify with the insurer or regulator before quoting):

| Case | Fine |
|---|---|
| New entrant, not insured within 15 days | AED 200 |
| New entrant, after 30 days | AED 300 |
| New entrant, each further month | AED 300 per month until insured |
| Renewal, not insured within the 30-day grace period | AED 300, repeating monthly |

Ambiguity: "after 30 days" and "per month thereafter" are both AED 300, so the start of the monthly cycle is not defined. Use this as a talking point for urgency in [[Negotiation stage stalls]] only when accurate. The document set for the same cases is in [[Policy submission documents]].
