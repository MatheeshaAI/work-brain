# Claims handling guide

UAE health insurance claims guide covering types, processes, rejections, and terminology. See [[Health Insurance Claims — UAE Guide]].

## Claim types
- Cashless (direct billing) - network hospitals, needs pre-auth except emergencies
- Reimbursement - pay first, claim back
- Pre-authorization - required for planned admissions/high-cost procedures
- Emergency - retrospective pre-auth may apply

## Common rejections
Pre-auth not obtained, non-network, missing docs, treatment not covered, sublimits exceeded, waiting period not completed.

## Processes
Planned admission (cashless): pre-auth → approval → admission → direct billing. Emergency: go to hospital/call insurer within 24-48h. Reimbursement: pay, collect docs, submit within 14-30 days.
