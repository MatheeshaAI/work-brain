# Group health insurance document checklist

For placing group health policies (the example was clinics), from Henry memory 2026-03-16. Henry turned it into a client email with a six-item "Please arrange to send us" list.

- **Company papers:** trade licence, establishment card, VAT certificate.
- **Staff data:** census list in the TPA's format, salary declaration, MOL (labour ministry) list.
- **Quote acceptance:** signed quote and table of benefits with the exclusion list.
- **Continuity:** certificate of continuity from the previous insurer.
- **Medical application form:** needed for larger groups (about 80+ members for the larger-salary band, 65+ for the lower band, per the note); **group critical illness form** for groups of 50 or fewer.
- **Free zone companies:** MOA/AOA.
- **Regulated or high-risk industries:** KYC form plus UBO ID documents (passport, Emirates ID or national ID of the Ultimate Beneficial Owner) - money exchange, real estate, jewellery, accounting, legal.
- **Renewals with a change of source:** exclusive broker-of-record letter.
