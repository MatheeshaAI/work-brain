# Underwriting rules

The rules that decide whether a case can be quoted at all, and on what terms. Three of them live in the workspace knowledge base, dated April 2026: one industry-wide, two insurer-specific.

## The over-65 medical gate
Members **above 65** need a completed medical report / pre-underwriting form, with eight diagnostic tests attached, before insurers will offer terms. The requirement for age exactly 65 is flagged as unclear in the source, so the desk applies it above 65 to be safe. Full field list and test list: [[General underwriting rules]].

The gate is **conditional in practice, not absolute**: the lead dossiers record tests as required only when there are no declared conditions *and* the member is over 65. Declared conditions pull in a different evidence route - medical reports and prescriptions per condition.

## Insurer eligibility conditions
Not every plan takes every child. On the Dubai Insurance Ramento plans a **child-alone policy (ages 6-12)** is accepted only if a sponsor condition holds: a parent insured with any insurer, both parents insured with any insurer, or one parent insured with Dubai Insurance on the same or a higher plan - with the sponsor's policy copy uploaded in each case. See [[Dubai Insurance underwriting notes]].

The pattern is worth keeping: **eligibility gates are plan-family specific, not insurer-wide**. The note says nothing about the other plans that insurer writes.

## Payment-linked issuance delay
At Takaful Emarat a **bank transfer takes one business day** to reflect, and documents are issued only once the payment has confirmed. The rule's real content is a client expectation: the policy will not be issued on the same day. See [[Takaful Emarat underwriting notes]].

## What the rules are not
- **They are not pricing rules.** Nothing here says a condition raises a premium; loadings come back in the revised quote, as in [[Qatar Insurance Company]].
- **They are not verified citations.** Every note is a desk note with no insurer document attached, and all three are dated April 2026.
- **They are not complete.** The knowledge base index lists a fourth note - a client-scoped plan scrape - whose file is missing from the folder. See [[Knowledge base index]].
