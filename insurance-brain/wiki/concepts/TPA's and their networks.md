# TPA's and their networks

TPAs operate hospital/provider networks; insurers underwrite plans. Networks and tiers are detailed in [[Network tiers]] and [[TPA and network reference]].

## Network structure (from [[TPA's and Their Networks with Structure]])
| TPA | Networks |
|---|---|
| NAS | NAS_CN, NAS_GN, NAS_RN, NAS_SRN, NAS_WN |
| MEDNET | MEDNET_GOLD, MEDNET_SILVER PREMIUM, MEDNET_SILVER CLASSIC, MEDNET_EMERALD, MEDNET_PEARL, MEDNET_SILK ROAD_IP, MEDNET_SILK ROAD_OP |
| NEXTCARE | NEXTCARE_GN_PLUS, NEXTCARE_GN, NEXTCARE_GN_LIMITED, NEXTCARE_RN, NEXTCARE_RN2, NEXTCARE_RN3 |
| DUBAICARE | DUBAICARE_N1, DUBAICARE_EXCLUSIVE N2, DUBAICARE_N2, DUBAICARE_N3, DUBAICARE_N4, DUBAICARE_N5 |
| ADNIC | ADNIC_PLATINUM, ADNIC_GOLD, ADNIC_SILVER, ADNIC_BRONZE |
| SUKOON | SUKOON_PREMIUM, SUKOON_EDGE, SUKOON_SIGNATURE_+_MEDCARE, SUKOON_SIGNATURE, SUKOON_ADVANCE, SUKOON_VITAL |
| ECARE | ECARE_CLASSIC, ECARE_GREEN, ECARE_BLUE |
| AAFİYA | AAFIYA_APN PLUS, AAFIYA_APN, AAFIYA_ESSENTIAL |

## Notes
- Counts differ across sources (design vs provider panels) - see [[TPA and network reference]].
- Provider panels (actual hospital lists) are the authority for coverage checks.
- TPA and insurer roles can appear together on quotes ([[Network tiers]]).
