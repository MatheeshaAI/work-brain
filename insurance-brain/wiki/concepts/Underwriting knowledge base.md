# Underwriting knowledge base

Underwriting knowledge base with insurer-specific rules. See [[Knowledge Base Index and Underwriting Notes]].

## Rules
- **Age over 65**: medical report/pre-underwriting form required, 8 tests (CBC, FBS, lipid, kidney/liver, ECG stress, abdomen/pelvis US, PSA for males), reports from UAE facility within last 3 months, pre-existing declaration important.
- **Dubai Insurance child-alone**: ages 6-12 only; requires sponsor parent insured with any insurer or both parents insured or one parent insured with Dubai Insurance on same/higher plan; policy copy required.
- **Takaful Emarat**: bank transfer takes 1 business day to reflect; policy issued after confirmation (~24 hours delay).
