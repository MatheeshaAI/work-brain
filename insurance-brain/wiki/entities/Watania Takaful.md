# Watania Takaful

**Watania International Holding** (formerly Noor Takaful / Dar Al Takaful) is a major UAE Islamic insurance provider. In health insurance, Watania offers a balanced product catalogue split between **[[Mednet]]** and **[[NAS]]** TPA networks.

In the workspace plan catalogue ([[Plans - Watania Takaful]]), Watania lists **12 plans** categorized into 3 Entry Level, 4 Good, and 5 Best.

---

## 1. Networks & Plan Structure

1. **MEDNET Suite (6 plans)**: Uses Mednet gem names:
   - `Silk Road` (Entry: AED 3,143.00)
   - `Emerald`, `Green` (Good: AED 3,957.00 to AED 4,909.00)
   - `Silver Classic`, `Silver Premium`, `Gold` (Best: AED 5,917.00 to AED 9,144.00)
2. **NAS Suite (6 plans)**: Numbered Plan 6 to Plan 1:
   - `Plan 5`, `Plan 6` (Entry: AED 2,568.00 to AED 3,091.00)
   - `Plan 4`, `Plan 3` (Good: AED 3,991.00 to AED 5,086.00)
   - `Plan 2`, `Plan 1` (Best: AED 8,629.00 to AED 10,747.00)

## 3. Co-Pay & Underwriting Specifics
- **Outpatient Consultation**: 20% co-insurance capped at **AED 75.00** per visit (higher consultation ceiling than the AED 50 market norm).
- **Diagnostics**: **10% co-insurance** on lab and imaging across all plans (lower out-of-pocket than the market standard 20%).
- **Prescription Medicines**: **10% co-insurance** on Mednet plans and **15% co-insurance** on NAS plans (significantly better than the typical 20% or 30% co-pays of budget carriers).

---

## 4. Market Position & Sales Notes
- **Favorable Co-Pay Trade-Off**: Watania charges higher upfront premiums than [[NLGIC Liva]] or [[Adamjee]] on the same networks, but rewards policyholders with lower co-pays on diagnostic tests and medicines.
- **Top-End NAS Ceiling**: `Plan 1` at AED 10,747 is one of the highest priced commercial SME plans on NAS outside dedicated IPMI products.
