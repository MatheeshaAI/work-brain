# Orient Takaful Insurance

**Orient Takaful PJSC** is the Sharia-compliant Islamic insurance subsidiary of the Al-Futtaim Group's [[Orient Insurance]] PJSC. In health insurance, it markets a streamlined series of numbered plans on its own network designation (**orient takaful**).

In the workspace plan catalogue ([[Plans - Orient Takaful]]), it lists **6 plans** categorized into 3 Good and 3 Best tiers.

---

## 1. Networks & Plan Structure

All 6 plans operate under the **orient takaful** network label, stepping up numerically:
- **Good Tier**: `PLAN5` (AED 1,561.38), `PLAN4` (AED 3,863.45), `PLAN3` (AED 4,237.24).
- **Best Tier**: `PLAN2L` (AED 5,899.31), `Plan 2` (AED 7,011.03), `Plan 1` (AED 7,788.97).

## 3. Co-Pay & Underwriting Specifics
- **Outpatient Consultation**: 20% co-insurance capped at **AED 50.00** per visit.
- **Diagnostics**: 20% co-insurance on laboratory and imaging.
- **Prescription Medicines**: 20% co-insurance on pharmacy.

---

## 4. Market Position & Label Anomalies
- **Plan 5 Pricing vs Classification**: `PLAN5` is priced at **AED 1,561.38** (AED 1,678.30 with VAT), which is an entry-level price point identical to [[Dubai Insurance]] Next N5 or [[Orient Insurance]] RN3, yet the CRM categorizes it as "Good".
- **Middle-Market Sharia Option**: Positioned for small business owners and families requiring Islamic compliance with competitive pricing.
