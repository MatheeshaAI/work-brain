# ISONSECURE

**ISONSECURE** is a specialized health insurance provider operating on its own proprietary provider network label (**ISONSECURE**).

In the workspace plan catalogue ([[Plans - ISONSECURE]]), it features **7 distinct plans** categorized into 4 Good and 3 Best tiers.

---

## 1. Plan Structure & Networks

All 7 plans utilize the **ISONSECURE** network label, structured across Classic Bronze, Classic Silver, Classic Gold, and Platinum ranges. Every plan in the Classic series includes integrated routine dental and optical benefits as standard.

## 3. Co-Pay & Underwriting Specifics
- **Outpatient Consultation**: 20% co-insurance capped at **AED 50.00** across Classic plans; **0% co-pay** on Platinum.
- **Diagnostics & Pharmacy**: **0% co-insurance** on lab/imaging and 0% on prescription medicines across all tiers.
- **Integrated Dental & Optical**: Classic plans include dental coverage at 20% co-insurance and optical coverage at 10% co-insurance.
- **Platinum Plan**: Complete zero-copay luxury plan (AED 16,163.38 base, AED 17,009.68 with VAT).

---

## 4. Market Position & Sales Notes
- **Zero-Copay Diagnostics/Pharmacy**: Unique in the mid-tier market for offering 0% co-pays on diagnostics and pharmacy while bundling dental/optical.
- **No Entry Level Offerings**: Starts at AED 5,590.30, focusing purely on comprehensive mid-to-high benefit seekers.
