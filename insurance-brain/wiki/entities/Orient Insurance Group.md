# Orient Insurance Group

The Orient companies are one group with subsidiaries. They keep separate pages because they write different products on different networks.

- [[Orient Insurance]]: the conventional insurer.
- [[Orient Takaful Insurance]]: the Shariah-compliant subsidiary.
- [[Orient-Allianz Insurance]]: the subsidiary that sells Allianz Partners international plans (Sphera).

Group relationship confirmed by the owner, 2026-10-28. Orient Takaful's page also places it in the Al-Futtaim group.
