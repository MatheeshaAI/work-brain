# Orient-Allianz Insurance

**Orient-Allianz** is a strategic underwriting partnership combining the local corporate strength of [[Orient Insurance]] PJSC with the international medical network of **Allianz Partners**. It distributes the acclaimed **Sphera** international private medical insurance product range on the **Allianz** network.

In the workspace plan catalogue ([[Plans - Orient-Allianz]]), Orient-Allianz lists **17 plans**, all classified in the **Best** price tier.

---

## 1. Networks & Plan Structure

All 17 plans operate on the **Allianz** international network, structured across five Core Sphera benefit tiers with modular Outpatient and Dental options:
- **Sphera Essence**: Baseline international plan (AED 11,271.42 to AED 16,839.26).
- **Sphera Base**: Mid-tier international plan (AED 13,327.58 to AED 19,101.44).
- **Sphera Enhanced**: Upper-mid international plan (AED 15,357.51 to AED 20,442.92).
- **Sphera Signature**: Executive international plan (AED 19,659.50 to AED 25,699.20).
- **Dubai Prime Silver (Comprehensive) Dental 3**: Regional luxury plan (AED 27,128.76).

## 3. Co-Pay & Underwriting Specifics
- **Outpatient Consultation**: 20% co-insurance across outpatient visits.
- **Diagnostics**: **NIL / 0% co-pay** on all diagnostic lab and radiology investigations.
- **Prescription Medicines**: 30% co-insurance on outpatient prescription pharmaceuticals.
- **Dental Modules (Dental 1 to 3)**: Progressively higher annual restorative and routine dental limits.

## 4. Market Position & Sales Notes
- **Direct IPMI Competitor**: Competes directly with [[Cigna Insurance]], [[April Hayah Insurance]], and [[BUPA Insurance]] for multinational executives and senior management.
- **Zero Low-End Products**: Lowest price in the catalogue is AED 11,271.42; Orient-Allianz serves clients seeking global portability and comprehensive inpatient hospital direct billing.
