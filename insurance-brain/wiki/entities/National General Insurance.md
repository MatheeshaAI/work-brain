# National General Insurance

**National General Insurance PJSC (NGI)** is a prominent Dubai-based listed national composite insurer. In health insurance, NGI manages its healthcare network and claims administration via the **[[HealthNet]]** TPA network.

In the workspace plan catalogue ([[Plans - National General Insurance]]), NGI lists **10 plans** categorized into 4 Entry Level, 4 Good, and 2 Best.

---

## 1. Plan Structure & Networks

All 10 plans operate on the **[[HealthNet]]** network and include integrated enhanced dental benefits:
- **Classic 4R / 4 Enhanced (Entry Level)**: AED 3,392.00 to AED 3,726.00.
- **Classic 3R / 3 Enhanced (Entry Level)**: AED 4,584.00 to AED 5,062.00.
- **Classic 2R / 2 Enhanced (Good)**: AED 5,832.00 to AED 6,458.00.
- **Classic 1R / 1 Enhanced (Good)**: AED 7,258.00 to AED 8,035.00.
- **Prime 2 / 1 Enhanced (Best)**: AED 9,541.00 to AED 11,386.00.

"R" designations signify restricted network options within the HealthNet network family.

## 3. Co-Pay & Underwriting Specifics
- **Outpatient Consultation**: 20% co-insurance capped at **AED 100.00** per visit across all tiers.
- **Diagnostics & Pharmacy**: **0% co-insurance** on lab, diagnostics, and prescription medicines across all plans.
- **Dental Benefit Included**: Enhanced dental is built directly into all Classic and Prime tiers.

---

## 4. Market Position & Sales Notes
- **High Consultation Cap Traded Against Zero Pharmacy/Lab**: NGI offsets its higher consultation cap (AED 100 vs standard AED 50) by charging 0% on medicines and diagnostic testing.
- **Elevated Entry Floor**: Starting at AED 3,392.00 base, NGI does not participate in the budget low-wage labor market, targeting higher-bracket dependents and SME professionals.
