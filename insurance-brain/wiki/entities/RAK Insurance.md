# RAK Insurance

**Ras Al Khaimah National Insurance Company (RAK Insurance)** is an established UAE composite insurer headquartered in Ras Al Khaimah with major branch operations in Dubai and Abu Dhabi. In health insurance, RAK Insurance deploys a tri-network strategy across **[[Mednet]]** (FCP series), **[[Nextcare]]**, and **RAK NAS** (custom [[NAS]] network).

In the workspace plan catalogue ([[Plans - RAK Insurance]]), RAK Insurance lists **18 plans** categorized into 5 Entry Level, 7 Good, and 6 Best.

---

## 1. Networks & Plan Structure

1. **MEDNET FCP Series (7 plans)**: Graded from `FCP Silk Road` up to `FCP Gold` (AED 2,671 to AED 7,765).
2. **NEXTCARE Suite (6 plans)**: From `RN3` and `Workers OP` up to `GN` (AED 1,617.14 to AED 7,209.52).
3. **RAK NAS Suite (5 plans)**: Custom NAS network tiers from `SRN` up to `CN` (AED 3,437 to AED 8,626).

## 3. Co-Pay & Underwriting Specifics
- **Mednet & NAS Co-Pay Formula**: Features a unique deductible structure: **20% deductible up to a maximum of AED 75.00** per visit, combined with **0% co-pay on outpatient pharmacy**.
- **Nextcare Co-Pay Formula**: Standard **20% co-insurance capped at AED 50.00** on consultation, 20% on diagnostics, and 20% on medicines.

---

## 4. Market Position & Sales Notes
- **Zero Pharmacy Co-Pay Appeal**: The Mednet FCP series is highly attractive to clients with ongoing chronic prescription needs, as medicines are covered at 100% (0% co-pay).
- **Distinction from Visa Emirate**: RAK Insurance as a carrier should not be confused with the "Ras Al Khaimah" emirate of visa field frequently recorded in lead files.
