# Health insurance

The business domain the shared brain is built around: the owner sells health insurance in the UAE, through [[IMCRM]] as the CRM and [[WhatsApp Business]] for follow-up.

## As a routed domain
`context/health-insurance/` is one of the six domain folders, holding industry knowledge, product information, sales scripts, objection handling, client management and claims, with a routing trigger list of "insurance sales strategy", "client objections", "sales scripts" and "product/competitor research". The folder is currently empty - see [[Context Routing Index and Domain Folders]].

## As the work itself
- Leads arrive through IMCRM, are prioritised by status and intent, and are followed up on WhatsApp. See [[Lead prioritization]] and [[IMCRM Health Leads May 2026]].
- The reply style for clients is fixed: concise, one clear next step. See [[Client message style]].
- The pipeline runs quote, confirmation, documents, payment. See [[Lead tracking in the CRM]] and [[Insurance CRM pipeline]].
- The networks and plans side of the domain is already covered elsewhere in the wiki: [[Mednet]], [[NAS]], [[Nextcare]], [[Dubai Care]], [[Takaful Emarat]], [[Network tiers]], [[Plan price tiers]], [[Customer categories]], [[Customer objections]], [[Explaining co-pay and plan terms]].
- The **money** side of the domain is in the career area: the health-insurance commission scheme, its tier table and the method that applies it. See [[Commission structure]], [[Commission calculation]], [[Insurancemarket.ae]]. The product line matters to the scheme because **group and corporate business enters the revenue blend at 2-7%** against 14-15% for individual and family plans, so the same product has very different value in the tier table.

## Status and caveats

- The shared brain holds no product or pricing knowledge of its own.
