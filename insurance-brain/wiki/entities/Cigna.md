# Cigna

**Cigna Healthcare Middle East** is a premier global health service company with extensive operations across the GCC. In the UAE, Cigna provides comprehensive local and expatriate medical insurance operating on the **[[Neuron]]** TPA network.

In the workspace plan catalogue ([[Plans - Cigna Insurance]]), Cigna features **40 plans**, all classified in the **Best** price tier.

---

## 1. Plan Structure & Networks

All 40 plans operate on the **[[Neuron]]** network and follow a modular tripartite framework:
1. **Regional**: Focused on the UAE, GCC, and broader Middle East region (AED 12,433.69 to AED 23,036.26).
2. **International**: Comprehensive global coverage with Worldwide Excluding USA (WWE) or Worldwide (WW) options (AED 13,321.81 to AED 37,760.36).
3. **International Plus**: Enhanced maximum benefit ceilings and international medical evacuation (AED 14,802.00 to AED 45,576.23).

### Add-on Modules & Specific Exclusions:
- **American Hospital Exclusion (`excl. AH`)**: Select plans exclude American Hospital Dubai from direct billing to reduce annual premiums by AED 1,000 to AED 2,200.
- **Healthy Connect**: Wellness and preventative healthcare rider integrated across select tiers.

## 3. Co-Pay & Underwriting Specifics
- **Outpatient Consultation**: Standardized **20% co-insurance capped at AED 100.00** per visit across all 40 plans (a higher cap than the standard AED 50 market norm).
- **Diagnostics & Pharmacy**: Covered according to network level and plan specifications with high annual limits.
- **Hospital Selection**: Direct billing at major tertiary hospital networks across the UAE, with flexible cost controls via the `excl. AH` option.
