# Fidelity United Insurance

**United Fidelity Insurance Company (Fidelity United)** is a prominent UAE national insurer. In the workspace, Fidelity United is recognized as a major writer of entry-level individual health insurance on the **[[Nextcare]]** TPA network.

In the workspace plan catalogue ([[Plans - Fidelity United]]), Fidelity United features **7 plans** alphabetically ordered from `Plan G` (cheapest entry) to `Plan A` (top tier).

---

## 1. Networks & Plan Structure

All 7 plans operate exclusively on the **[[Nextcare]]** network, graded across three functional price tiers:
- **Entry Level (Plans G, F, E)**: AED 1,163.00 to AED 1,433.00.
- **Good (Plans D, C)**: AED 3,631.00 to AED 3,979.00.
- **Best (Plans B, A)**: AED 6,567.00 to AED 7,294.00.

## 3. Co-Pay & Underwriting Specifics
- **Outpatient Consultation**: 20% co-insurance capped at **AED 50.00** per visit.
- **Diagnostics**: 20% co-insurance on laboratory and radiology tests.
- **Prescription Medicines**: **30% co-insurance on medicines** across Entry Level plans (G, F, E) and 20% on higher tiers. The 30% pharmacy co-pay is higher than the standard 20% market rate, which enables Fidelity United to lower the upfront premium.

## 4. Market Position & Sales Notes
- **Extremely Competitive Entry Floor**: At AED 1,163.00 (AED 1,260.00 with VAT), `Plan G` is the third-cheapest plan in the entire 418-plan library, competing directly with [[Takaful Emarat Insurance]] APN (AED 1,114) and [[Qatar Insurance Company]] BLUE01 (AED 1,186).
- **Filter Inconsistency**: Fidelity United is sometimes omitted by automated basic plan scraping tools due to strict network filtering, despite being featured prominently in the workspace's [[Entry Level Plan guide]].
