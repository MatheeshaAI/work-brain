# Daman Insurance

**National Health Insurance Company - Daman** is the UAE's premier government-backed health insurer (majority-owned by PureHealth / ADQ). The dominant market leader in Abu Dhabi, Daman also distributes individual and corporate health insurance in Dubai and the Northern Emirates on its proprietary **Daman Insurance** network.

In the workspace plan catalogue ([[Plans - Daman]]), Daman features **9 individual plans**, categorized into 2 Entry Level, 2 Good, and 5 Best.

---

## 1. Networks & Plan Hierarchy

All 9 plans operate on the **Daman Insurance** network. Plans follow Daman's recognized metal categories with Dubai/Northern Emirates (DNE) network designations and optional dental add-ons:
- **Care Bronze DNE DM5**: Entry Level designation (AED 10,392.00 to AED 10,464.00).
- **Care Silver DNE DM5**: Good designation (AED 12,335.00 to AED 12,407.00).
- **Care Gold DNE DM5**: Best designation (AED 15,916.00 to AED 16,300.00).
- **Care Platinum DNE DM5**: Best designation (AED 21,798.00 to AED 22,238.00).
- **Premier DNE Individual**: Ultra-luxury designation (AED 45,506.00).

## 3. Co-Pay & Underwriting Specifics
- **Outpatient Consultation**: 20% co-insurance capped at **AED 50.00** across Care Bronze, Silver, Gold, Platinum, and Premier.
- **Diagnostics & Pharmacy**: **10% co-insurance** across laboratory tests, radiology, and prescription medicines.
- **Dental Riders**: Dental add-on options add a minor incremental premium (e.g. AED 72 on Bronze/Silver; AED 384-440 on Gold/Platinum).

## 4. Market Position & Label Contradiction
- **The AED 10k Entry Floor Contradiction**: In CRM plan categorization ([[CRM plan type labels]]), Daman's Care Bronze is labelled "Entry Level" despite costing **AED 10,392.00**, because it represents Daman's internal entry tier. In market reality, this price exceeds almost every other insurer's "Best" tier.
- **Reputational Strength**: Renowned for regulatory compliance, prompt pre-authorization, and extensive direct provider coverage across Abu Dhabi and Dubai.
