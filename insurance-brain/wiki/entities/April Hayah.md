# April Hayah

**April Hayah** is a specialized international health insurance partnership between global IPMI specialist **April International** and UAE local carrier [[HAYAH Insurance]]. Operating on the [[Mednet]] TPA network, April Hayah focuses exclusively on premium expatriate individuals, families, and executives requiring cross-border inpatient and outpatient coverage.

In the workspace plan library ([[Plans - April Hayah]]), April Hayah features **28 distinct plans**, all classified in the **Best** price tier.

---

## 1. Plan Architecture & Networks

All 28 plans operate under the **[[Mednet]]** network framework, split across Mednet's network tiers:
- **Mednet Green**: Entry-level IPMI options (AED 14,388.86 to AED 30,836.56).
- **Mednet Silver**: Mid-tier IPMI options (AED 17,266.63 to AED 29,038.57).
- **Mednet Premium**: Top-tier executive options (AED 25,123.01 to AED 42,487.15).

Coverage zones are modular:
- **WWEx**: Worldwide excluding USA.
- **WW**: Worldwide including USA.

### Benefit Modules:
Plans combine three distinct building blocks:
- **Inpatient (IP)**: Core, Essential, Comfort, or Premium.
- **Outpatient (OP)**: Essential or Extensive.
- **Dental**: Essential Dental or Extensive Dental.

## 3. Co-Pay & Underwriting Specifics
- **Outpatient Consultation**: 20% co-insurance capped at an exact **AED 51.00** per visit across all comprehensive tiers.
- **Diagnostics & Pharmacy**: 20% co-insurance applies across both lab/radiology and prescription medicines.
- **Worldwide Flexibility**: Provides emergency and elective overseas medical treatment depending on the WWEx vs Worldwide designation.

---

## 4. Market Position & Sales Observations
- **Top-Tier IPMI**: Competing directly against [[BUPA]], [[Cigna]], and [[Orient-Allianz]] for senior corporate executives and high-net-worth expat residents.
- **Zero Low-End Products**: April Hayah provides no entry-level or budget plans; its lowest priced option (Core Inpatient) is AED 9,425.09.
- **Relation to Local Carrier**: Operates distinct from HAYAH's domestic Nextcare products ([[HAYAH Insurance]]), leveraging April International's underwriting algorithms and Mednet's medical billing rails.
