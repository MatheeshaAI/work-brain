# Abu Dhabi National Takaful Insurance

**Abu Dhabi National Takaful** is the Sharia-compliant sister company of [[Abu Dhabi National Insurance Company]] (ADNIC). In the health insurance workspace, it operates entirely on the **[[NAS]]** TPA network, offering an extensive structured hierarchy across NAS provider networks.

In the workspace plan catalogue ([[Plans - Abu Dhabi National Takaful]]), it features **30 distinct plans** categorized into 5 Entry Level, 11 Good, and 14 Best.

---

## 1. Network Progression & Plan Structure

All 30 plans utilize the **[[NAS]]** network, mapping directly to NAS's standard network codes:
- **WN (Workers Network)**: Entry-level plans (`Care- WN`, `Care- WN-a/b/c`, `Core`), AED 2,228 to AED 2,649.
- **SRN (Standard Restricted Network)**: Lower-mid tier (`Care- SRN`, `Care- SRN-a/b/c`), AED 3,915 to AED 4,603.
- **RN (Restricted Network)**: Mid-tier Good plans (`Care- RN`, `Care- RN-a/b/c`, `Dubai 3`), AED 4,773 to AED 5,655.
- **GN (General Network)**: Comprehensive Best plans (`Care- GN`, `Care- GN-a/b/c/d`, `Dubai 1`), AED 7,805 to AED 9,187.
- **CN (Comprehensive Network)**: Executive tier (`Care- CN`, `Care- CN-a/b/c/d`), AED 10,766 to AED 12,381.
- **IPMI**: International private medical insurance tiers (`IPMI 1`, `IPMI 2` Worldwide ex. US / Worldwide), AED 20,579 to AED 29,273.

## 3. Co-Pay & Underwriting Specifics
- **Outpatient Consultations**:
  - Standard options: 20% capped at AED 50.00.
  - Sub-option variants (`-a`, `-d`): 10% co-pay capped at AED 25.00 or tiered (AED 35 for GP, AED 50 for Specialist).
- **Diagnostics**:
  - Baseline WN/SRN: 20% co-insurance.
  - Higher-tier options (`-a` and `-b`): 0% or 10% on laboratory and radiology.
- **Prescription Medicines**:
  - Range from 30% co-insurance on basic plans (`Core`) down to 0% on top GN/CN variants.
- **Letter Variants**: Suffix letters (`a`, `b`, `c`, `d`) represent decreasing co-pay burdens for a higher base premium.

## 4. Market Position & Sales Notes
- **Clean Tiered Ladder**: Offers one of the most transparent network upgrade paths on NAS, from low-wage labor (`WN`) to high-end global executive (`IPMI Worldwide`).
- **Absence in Commission Book**: Like ADNIC, Abu Dhabi National Takaful has no recorded policies in the sales commission exports, serving primarily as a comparative quote benchmark.
