# BUPA Insurance

**British United Provident Association (BUPA)** is a leading international private medical insurance (IPMI) provider. In the UAE market, BUPA operates in local regulatory partnership with **[[Sukoon Insurance]]** (formerly Oman Insurance Company), utilizing Sukoon's premium healthcare provider networks.

In the workspace plan catalogue ([[Plans - BUPA]]), BUPA lists **5 exclusive plans**, all categorized in the **Best** price tier.

---

## 1. Networks & Plan Structure

BUPA products operate across two Sukoon network tiers:
1. **SUKOON SIGNATURE + MEDCARE**: Regional and Worldwide plans offering full access to premium private hospital groups including Medcare.
2. **SUKOON PREMIUM**: Executive tiers (`Premier`, `Elite`, `Ultimate`) providing VIP medical access across the UAE and internationally.

## 3. Co-Pay & Underwriting Specifics
- **Premier, Elite, and Select Plans**: Standard 20% co-insurance across outpatient consultations, laboratory diagnostics, and prescription pharmaceuticals.
- **BUPA Ultimate**: Features **NIL (0%) co-pay** on outpatient consultations, diagnostics, and prescription medicines. Offers completely frictionless private healthcare with comprehensive worldwide limits.
- **Price Spectrum**: Ranging from AED 14,454.96 (Select Regional) up to **AED 102,498.01** (AED 107,661.76 with VAT) for BUPA Ultimate.
