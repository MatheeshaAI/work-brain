# QIC

An insurer that appears in the [[Daily sales audit]] window as the source of a **revised quote**. This page was a stub built from the audit notes; the plan library has since been ingested.

- On 27 May the insurer sent a quote revision after a medical review; the terms had changed. On 28 and 29 May it was still unreviewed and unshared with the client, and the audit's instruction was to **call before sending anything by email** rather than forward the revised document cold.
- It was being quoted **against** an incumbent insurer in the same escalated case, alongside an outstanding request for a final figure. The competing quote was the thing blocking that sale.
- The revised quote is recorded as carrying a medical loading.

## The plan library (2026-10-22 ingest)
**19 plans** - Entry Level 13, Good 4, Best 2 - priced **AED 1,186 to 7,281** before VAT, on **two** network labels: *Qatar Insurance Company* (its own) and **QIC MEDNET**. The plan family is the BLUE series (BLUE01, BLUE02, BLUE03 and upwards). Detail: [[Plans - Qatar Insurance Company]], [[Insurance plan sheets]].

**The entry-level weighting is the point.** Two thirds of its plans sit in the entry band, and the cheapest plan in the whole library belongs to a different insurer - so QIC is a cheap-quote writer with almost no top-tier product. When a client asks for a Best-tier plan, this insurer is rarely the answer. See [[Plan price tiers]] and [[Network tiers]].
