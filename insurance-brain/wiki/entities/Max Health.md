# Max Health

**Max Health** is an executive health insurance provider in the UAE operating under the **Maxwell** provider network label.

In the workspace plan catalogue ([[Plans - Max Health]]), Max Health features **3 high-end plans**, all categorized in the **Best** price tier.

---

## 1. Plan Structure & Networks

All 3 plans operate on the **Maxwell** network, designed for corporate executives, partners, and high-coverage individuals:
- **ADVANCED**: AED 12,949.00 (AED 13,635.30 with VAT).
- **PRESTIGE**: AED 15,461.00 (AED 16,272.90 with VAT).
- **ELITE**: AED 16,716.00 (AED 17,590.65 with VAT).

## 3. Co-Pay & Underwriting Specifics
- **Outpatient Consultation**:
  - `ADVANCED` and `PRESTIGE`: Flat **AED 50.00 deductible** per consultation (no percentage co-insurance).
  - `ELITE`: **0% / NIL co-pay** per consultation.
- **Diagnostics & Pharmacy**: **0% co-insurance** on all diagnostic laboratory tests, imaging, and prescription medicines across all three plans.

---

## 4. Market Position & Sales Notes
- **Frictionless Executive Product**: Attractive to clients seeking simple flat-fee consultations with zero pharmacy or laboratory out-of-pocket costs.
- **Narrow Niche**: With only 3 plans and zero budget tiers, Max Health represents a boutique alternative to larger IPMI carriers like [[Cigna]] or [[BUPA]].
