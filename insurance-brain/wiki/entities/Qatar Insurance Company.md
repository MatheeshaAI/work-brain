# Qatar Insurance Company

**Qatar Insurance Company (QIC)** is a prominent regional insurer in the GCC with significant UAE health insurance operations. In the workspace, QIC is known primarily as a high-volume writer of entry-level individual health insurance and is also documented under its short entity acronym [[QIC]].

In the workspace plan catalogue ([[Plans - Qatar Insurance Company]]), QIC lists **19 plans**, with 13 in Entry Level, 4 in Good, and 2 in Best.

---

## 1. Networks & Plan Structure

QIC distributes plans across two distinct network designations:
1. **Qatar Insurance Company Network**: A proprietary network used for the 8 budget **BLUE** plans (`BLUE01` to `BLUE08`) and 4 **CLASSIC** plans (`CLASSIC1` to `CLASSIC4`), priced between AED 1,186 and AED 2,550.
2. **QIC MEDNET**: A customized [[Mednet]] TPA network powering the numbered tiers (`PLAN7` down to `PLAN1_QIC`), spanning entry-level (PLAN7) up to comprehensive cover (PLAN1_QIC).

## 3. Co-Pay & Underwriting Specifics
- **Uniform Standard Co-Pay**: Every single plan in QIC's roster utilizes an identical co-pay formula: **20% co-insurance capped at AED 50.00** per outpatient consultation, **20%** on diagnostics (lab/radiology), and **20%** on prescription pharmacy.
- **Micro-Tiering in the BLUE Series**: Price jumps between BLUE plans are as narrow as AED 15 to AED 96, allowing advisors to calibrate exactly to a client's budget ceiling or visa requirement.
- **Medical Loadings & Underwriting Delays**: In late May 2026 sales audits ([[Daily sales audit]]), QIC was noted for issuing a revised quote with medical loading following health review, requiring proactive telephone follow-up rather than sending revised terms by email.

## 4. Sales Positioning & Market Share
- **Budget Pricing Competitor**: At AED 1,186 base (`BLUE01`), QIC offers the second-cheapest plan in the entire 418-plan library, undercut only by [[Takaful Emarat]]'s APN (AED 1,114) and competing directly with [[Fidelity United]]'s Plan G (AED 1,163).
- **Thin Commission Book**: Despite carrying 19 plans in the library, QIC represents only 3 out of 84 recorded commission lines in the desk's commission exports ([[Sales commission export]]), showing that brokers quote QIC primarily for extreme price-sensitive shoppers or visa compliance.
