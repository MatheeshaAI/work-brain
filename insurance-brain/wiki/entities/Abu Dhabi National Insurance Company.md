# Abu Dhabi National Insurance Company

**Abu Dhabi National Insurance Company (ADNIC)** is one of the largest and longest-established composite insurers in the UAE. In the health insurance sector, ADNIC markets its individual products under the **Shifa** plan brand on its own proprietary network.

In the workspace plan catalogue ([[Plans - Abu Dhabi National Insurance Company]]), ADNIC features **36 distinct plans**, categorized into 4 Entry Level, 7 Good, and 25 Best.

---

## 1. Plan Structure & Networks

All 36 plans sit on the insurer's own network (**Abu Dhabi National Insurance Company**), avoiding external third-party administrator (TPA) rails. Plans follow a four-metal tier structure:
- **Bronze (Entry Level)**: Options 1 to 4 (AED 3,178.00 to AED 3,935.00).
- **Silver (Good & Best)**: Standard Silver options 5, 9, 13, 14, Silver Dental options 2, 6, 10, and Silver 1.
- **Gold (Best)**: Options 1 to 22, including standalone, Dental add-ons, and comprehensive Dental & Optical bundles.

## 3. Co-Pay & Underwriting Specifics
- **Outpatient Consultations**: 20% co-insurance capped at either **AED 30.00** or **AED 50.00** depending on the specific option number.
- **Diagnostics**: 0% co-pay on laboratory and radiology across all tiers.
- **Prescription Medicines**: Co-pay scales inversely with plan price: Bronze entry plans require 20% co-insurance on pharmacy; Silver plans require 10% or 20%; Gold plans feature 0% pharmacy co-pay.
- **Dental & Optical Riders**: Dental options carry 20% co-insurance for routine procedures and 50% for major restorative treatments; optical riders feature 0% co-insurance.

## 4. Market Position & Sales Observations
- **Premium Entry Floor**: ADNIC's cheapest plan (`SHIFA BRONZE 4` at AED 3,178.00) costs nearly three times the baseline entry plans of [[Takaful Emarat]] (AED 1,114) or [[Fidelity United]] (AED 1,163), placing it in the mid-to-high personal visa bracket.
- **Sister Takaful Operator**: ADNIC operates alongside its Islamic window [[Abu Dhabi National Takaful]], which sells via [[NAS]].
- **Sales Presence**: The vault contains no direct commission rows or renewal files for ADNIC, indicating it is primarily a benchmark reference rather than a core high-conversion carrier for the desk.
