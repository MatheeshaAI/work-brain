# Cigna Healthguard

An international health insurance product line the owner sells. Facts recorded in [[Henry memory 2026-03-19]] (from the Cigna 2025 documents, via the [[Cigna agent]]):
- Three plans: International, Regional, International Plus.
- **Regional** covers GCC, Middle East and Asia excluding Singapore, Hong Kong and China; annual maximum AED 2,750,000; out-of-area emergency AED 180,000 (about 50,000 USD).
- Overseas treatment is reimbursed against medical reports and invoices; whether it is "fully" reimbursed was the open client question and the answer drafted cited the limits above, not unconditional reimbursement.
- Process pattern: after payment, policy documents come from Cigna and are forwarded to the client ([[Henry memory 2026-03-22 and 2026-03-23 (work log)]]).
