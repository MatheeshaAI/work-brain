# DIC-Viventa Insurance

**DIC-Viventa** represents a specialized product partnership between [[Dubai Insurance]] Company (DIC) and benefits administrator Viventa, marketed under the **Access Health BenefitsPlus** brand on the **[[Mednet]]** TPA network.

In the workspace plan catalogue ([[Plans - DIC-Viventa]]), it features **9 distinct plans** categorized into 7 Good and 2 Best.

---

## 1. Networks & Benefit Architecture

All 9 plans operate on the **[[Mednet]]** network framework, ascending through Mednet's standard network grades:
- **Silk Road**: Entry-mid baseline (AED 3,180.57).
- **Emerald**: Mid-tier network (AED 4,298.55 to AED 4,305.55).
- **Green**: Established mid-level network (AED 5,096.34 to AED 5,103.35).
- **Silver Classic & Premium**: Upper-tier network (AED 6,410.49 to AED 8,372.38).
- **Gold**: Comprehensive top-tier network (AED 9,428.20).

Numeric suffixes (`500`, `750`, `1000`) denote out-of-pocket pharmacy limits or deductible thresholds.

## 3. Co-Pay & Underwriting Specifics
- **Outpatient Consultation**: Standard **20% co-insurance capped at AED 50.00** per consultation.
- **Diagnostics**: 20% co-insurance across laboratory tests and imaging.
- **Prescription Medicines**: 20% co-insurance subject to plan-specific pharmacy caps.

---

## 4. Market Position & Sales Notes
- **Corporate & Executive Alternative**: Functions as Dubai Insurance's specialized Mednet program, distinct from DIC's primary Dubai Care and E-Care Blue lines.
- **Price Range**: Starting at AED 3,180.57 up to AED 9,428.20 (AED 9,938.46 with VAT), providing a structured step-up for SME staff and mid-market professionals.
