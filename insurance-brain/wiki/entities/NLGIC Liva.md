# NLGIC Liva

**National Life & General Insurance Company (NLGIC)**, operating under the rebranded regional insurance identity **Liva Group**, is an established Oman/UAE insurer. In the workspace, NLGIC Liva utilizes a dual-TPA approach, offering parallel product suites on both **[[Mednet]]** and **[[NAS]]**.

In the workspace plan catalogue ([[Plans - NLGIC Liva Insurance]]), NLGIC Liva lists **12 plans** categorized into 2 Entry Level, 6 Good, and 4 Best.

---

## 1. Networks & Plan Structure

1. **MEDNET Suite (7 plans)**: Follows Mednet's gem-based naming:
   - `Silk Road` (Entry: AED 2,521.22)
   - `PEARL`, `Emerald`, `Green`, `Silver Classic` (Good: AED 2,901.54 to AED 5,135.45)
   - `Silver Premium`, `Gold` (Best: AED 6,693.69 to AED 7,543.43)
2. **NAS Suite (5 plans)**: Numbered from Plan 5 to Plan 1:
   - `Plan 5` (Entry: AED 2,036.14 — cheapest NAS plan in the entire 418-plan library)
   - `Plan 4`, `Plan 3` (Good: AED 2,933.24 to AED 3,770.82)
   - `Plan 2`, `Plan 1` (Best: AED 6,676.10 to AED 7,410.22)

## 3. Co-Pay & Underwriting Specifics
- **Outpatient Consultation**: Standard 20% co-insurance capped at **AED 50.00** across both Mednet and NAS networks.
- **Diagnostics**: 20% co-insurance on lab and radiology.
- **Prescription Medicines**: 20% co-insurance on pharmacy.

---

## 4. Market Position & Sales Notes
- **Price Leader on NAS**: `Plan 5` at AED 2,036.14 is the lowest priced option among all NAS network offerings in the library, beating competitor NAS plans from [[Abu Dhabi National Takaful]] (AED 2,228), [[Watania Takaful]] (AED 2,568), and [[Adamjee]] (AED 2,685).
- **Aggressive Mednet Pricing**: Undercuts equivalent Mednet plans from [[Orient Insurance]] and [[Adamjee]] by roughly 5% to 10% across Silk Road, Pearl, and Emerald.
