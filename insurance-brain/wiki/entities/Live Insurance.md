# Live Insurance

**Stub.** On the renewals board one April renewal row names the provider as **Live Insurance**, with a Takaful Emarat policy number and a premium of 9,162.56 AED - the one renewal that was actually completed, consolidated into a combined family policy. Source: [[Renewals board]].

**It is [[Takaful Emarat]] under its retail name.** The policy number format `01-115-01-25-...` is Takaful Emarat's, and the renewal files in the same folder use the `TE-` prefix for the same shapes. Nothing in the workspace states the relationship in words, so this page records the inference rather than a fact.

**The practical point is the naming.** The same insurer appears in the workspace as *Takaful Emarat Insurance*, *TE*, and *Live Insurance* - in the commission exports, the renewal boards and the renewal filenames respectively. Any report that groups renewals by provider has to reconcile three labels for one insurer, or it will report a "Live Insurance" book of one policy that does not exist. See [[Renewal pipeline]] and [[CRM hygiene]].
