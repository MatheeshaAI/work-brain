## Entry Level Comparison: Price vs. Coverage

### Most Affordable
- **BLUE01** (Qatar Ins.) - 1,186 AED | 20% max AED 25 across all services
- **Fidelity Plan G** - 1,163 AED | 20% max AED 50 consultation

### Best Value (Low price + good coverage)
- **E Care Blue 1** - 1,209 AED | Balanced 20% co-pay with AED 25 cap
- **BLUE02** - 1,276 AED | Lower co-pays on diagnostics & medicines (10%)
- **Dubai Care Next N5B** - 1,450 AED | Higher cap (AED 50) with consistent 20% co-pay

### Premium Entry Level (Higher price, expanded benefits)
- **Silk Road** (various providers) - 2,600-2,900 AED | MEDNET access
- **NAS plans** - 2,000-3,400 AED | Government backing, strong facilities

## Selection Tips

**For Budget-Conscious:**
- Qatar Insurance Company plans (BLUE series) - Most affordable
- Takaful Emarat E Care Blue - Good value with flexibility

**For Better Coverage:**
- Fidelity Plans (E, F, G) on NEXTCARE
- Silk Road plans on MEDNET
- NAS family plans from Takaful Emarat/Abu Dhabi National Takaful

**For Large Families:**
- Silk Road & Pearl plans (MEDNET) - Better for family co-pays
- NAS plans - Family-friendly with government backing

**For UAE Citizens/Residents:**
- Abu Dhabi National Takaful plans
- Takaful Emarat (Islamic compliance)
- Dubai Insurance (premium network)
