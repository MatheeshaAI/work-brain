# Takaful Emarat — Underwriting Notes

## Bank Transfer — Policy Issuance Delay

**Applies to:** Bank transfer payments direct to Takaful Emarat

Bank transfers take **1 business day** to reflect in Takaful Emarat's system. Policy documents are only issued **after** the payment is confirmed on their end.

- Client pays via bank transfer → 24-hour processing window → policy documents issued
- Set client expectation upfront: policy will not be issued on the same day

*Noted: April 2026*
