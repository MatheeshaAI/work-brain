# UAE Health Insurance Plans — Complete Reference

## Plan Structure Overview

```
HEALTH INSURANCE PLANS
        │
┌───────┼────────────────────────┐
▼       ▼                        ▼
BASIC   BASIC ENHANCED          ENHANCED              INTERNATIONAL
(Clinic (More clinics +         (Hospital Coverage)   (Worldwide)
 only)   specialists)            │
                                 ├── Level 1 (Entry hospitals)
                                 ├── Level 2 (More hospitals)
                                 ├── Level 3 (Premium hospitals)
                                 ├── Level 4 (Top-tier hospitals)
                                 ├── Level 5 (Maximum options)
                                 └── Level 6 (Maximum options)
```

## Plan Tiers

| Tier | Name | Coverage | Price Level |
|------|------|----------|-------------|
| 1 | Basic | Clinic visits only (outpatient) | Lowest |
| 2 | Basic Enhanced | Extended clinics + specialist access | Low-Mid |
| 3 | Enhanced | Hospital (outpatient + inpatient), Levels 1-6 | Mid-High |
| 4 | International | Worldwide coverage | Highest |

## Each Plan Adds These Variables

| Variable | Options |
|---|---|
| TPA | Nextcare, MEDNET, eCare, Aafiya, NAS, DubaiCare, ADNIC, Sukoon |
| Co-payment | 0% / 10% / 20% |

> **Total variations: 100+ plan combinations**

## Co-Pay Impact

| Co-Pay | Premium | Customer Pays at Visit |
|--------|---------|------------------------|
| 0% | Highest | AED 0 |
| 10% | Medium | 10% of bill |
| 20% | Lowest | 20% of bill |
