# Required Documents For Policy Submission

## Required Documents
- Medical Application Form
- Member's Passport
- Member's Visa
- Member's Emirates ID
- Sponsor's Passport
- Sponsor's Visa
- Sponsor's Emirates ID

## For Company-Purchased Policies
When the insurance policy is purchased by the company for an employee:
- Trade License
- VAT Certificate
- Establishment Card

## For Newborns
When adding a newborn to the policy:
- Birth Certificate
- Discharge Summary (not required after 1 year)
- **If discharge summary is from home country:**
  - Fitness Certificate from pediatrician

## Notes
- All documents must be valid and up-to-date
- Original copies may be required for verification
- Additional documents may be requested based on specific insurance provider requirements
- Newborn-specific documents (birth certificate and discharge summary) are only required during the first year of coverage
- **Pre-existing conditions:** if any condition is declared, companies require supporting medical reports, prescriptions and treatment records from within the last 3 months in UAE
- **Members aged 75+ years:** a fitness certificate with relevant test reports is required by all insurers to proceed with a policy

## Visa-specific Requirements

### New Members to UAE (Dubai visa holders)
For policy issuance, insurance companies require the following documents in addition to the medical application form:
- Emirates ID application form
- E‑visa
- Passport
- Sponsor documents (if the member is a dependent)

### Northern Emirates Visa Holders
Only the change of status and the passport are required.
Sponsor documents are needed if the member is a dependent.

### Abu Dhabi Visa Holders
- Medical application form
- Passport and visa
- Emirates ID (if a renewal)
- Certificate of Continuity (COC) is mandatory for renewals.

### New Entrant Required Documents
- Change of status (or entry stamp if change of status is not available)
- Passport
- Sponsor documents if applicable

### Fines for Late Insurance
- **New entrants:**
  - AED 200 fine if insurance not obtained within 15 days
  - AED 300 after 30 days
  - AED 300 per month thereafter until insurance is purchased
- **Renewals:**
  - AED 300 fine if not insured within the 30‑day grace period; continues monthly until coverage is obtained
