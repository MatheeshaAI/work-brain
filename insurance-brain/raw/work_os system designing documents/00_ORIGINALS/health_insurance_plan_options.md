## Plan Tiers Overview

| Tier | Name | Coverage | Price Level |
|------|------|----------|-------------|
| 1 | Basic | Clinic visits only (outpatient) | Lowest |
| 2 | Basic Enhanced | Extended clinics + specialists | Low-Mid |
| 3 | Enhanced | Hospital (outpatient + inpatient) | Mid-High |
| 4 | International | Worldwide coverage | Highest |

---

## Enhanced Tier (Level 1-6)

```
ENHANCED LEVELS
═══════════════
Level 1  → Entry hospital access (fewest hospitals) → Lowest price
Level 2  → Expanded hospital network
Level 3  → Premium hospital network
Level 4  → Top-tier hospitals
Level 5  → Maximum hospital options
Level 6  → Maximum hospital options (highest in tier)
```

## Quick Product Summary (For Research/Explaining)

> **Product:** Individual & Family Health Insurance (UAE)
> 
> **Structure:** 4 Tiers × 6 Enhanced Levels × 5-6 TPAs × 3 Co-pay Options
> 
> **Total Variations:** 100+ plan combinations
> 
> **Key Variables:**
> - Coverage type (clinic/hospital/international)
> - Hospital network size (Enhanced levels 1-6)
