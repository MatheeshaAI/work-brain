# Sales Context Index

This folder stores stable reference context for sales work. Part of the broader [[Sales Index]] domain and [[Company Index]] context.

## Files
- [[browser-profile]] — which browser profile to use and how to use controlled tabs
- [[tools-to-use]] — preferred browser and extraction tools for sales tasks
- [[imcrm]] — IMCRM-specific knowledge, patterns, and extraction notes
- [[whatsapp]] — WhatsApp Business working rules and limits
- [[lead-handling-rules]] — how to prioritize and handle leads
- [[message-style]] — preferred communication style for client replies
- [[business-goals]] — sales and revenue targets
- [[do-not-do]] — safety and workflow limits

## What belongs here
- Stable working rules
- Tool choices
- CRM knowledge
- Communication preferences
- Lead handling rules

## What does not belong here
- Raw lead exports
- Monthly lead tables
- Temporary scraped output
- Daily follow-up lists

Those belong in:
- `../Leads/`
- `../Workflows/`
