# Message Style

## Preferred tone
- Concise
- Direct
- Professional
- Helpful without fluff

## Reply style
- Move the client toward the next step
- Keep replies easy to read
- Use simple language
- Avoid long paragraphs unless explanation is necessary

## Preferred next steps in replies
- confirm documents
- confirm quote
- schedule call
- send payment link
- ask one clear closing question

## Avoid
- overexplaining
- vague follow-ups
- passive wording
- overly formal corporate tone
