## Key Takeaways

- **Core idea:** People hate to be sold but love to buy. The goal is to make them feel like they're making the decision on their own.
- **The 10/10 framework:** Only sell to people who are at least an 8/10 convinced. Anything less and your job is to get them to 10/10 through genuine value — not manipulation.
- **Due Diligence Before Pitch:** Open a dedicated space *before* your pitch for the prospect to ask questions about you, your company, and your policies. This builds maximum trust so the pitch lands harder.
- **Rejection of traditional sales tactics:** No objection-handling tricks, no manipulative discounts, no pressure.
