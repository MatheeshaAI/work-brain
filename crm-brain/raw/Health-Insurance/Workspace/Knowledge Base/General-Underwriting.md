# General Underwriting — Industry-Wide Rules

## Age 65+ Medical Underwriting Requirements

**Applies to:** Members **above 65 years** (requirement for age 65 itself is unclear — apply when > 65 to be safe)

When quoting or booking a policy for a member above 65, insurance companies require a completed **Medical Report / Pre-Underwriting Form** before they can offer terms.

---

### Required Medical Form — Fields

The insurer's medical form covers:

**Member Details**
- Full Name, Date of Birth, Gender
- Nationality, Passport / Emirates ID No.

**Medical History** (tick all that apply, provide details)
- Diabetes Mellitus
- Hypertension
- Cardiovascular Disease
- Respiratory Disease
- Renal Disease
- Cancer (any type)
- Neurological / Psychiatric Disorders
- Other Chronic Illnesses

**Past History** — medical & surgical

**Current Medications**

**Physical Examination**
- Height, Weight, BMI
- Blood Pressure, Pulse
- General Examination Findings

**Treatment Plan**

---

### Required Laboratory / Diagnostic Tests

The following tests must be attached with the form (reports to be sourced by the member):

1. CBC (Complete Blood Count)
2. Fasting Blood Sugar
3. Complete Lipid Profile
4. Kidney Function Tests
5. Liver Function Tests
6. ECG Stress Test
7. Ultrasound — Abdomen and Pelvis
8. Prostate Specific Antigen *(males only)*

---

### Pre-Existing Conditions — Declaration

If a member has **pre-existing conditions** and wishes to **declare** them:

- Must provide **relevant medical reports** for each condition
- Must provide **current medication prescriptions**
- Declaration is optional — but if undisclosed, claims may be rejected

---

### Document Validity Rules

> ⚠️ **All medical reports and documents must:**
> - Be issued by a **UAE-based** medical facility
> - Be dated **within the last 3 months**

Reports from outside the UAE or older than 3 months will not be accepted by underwriters.
