# FTC Email Template

**Purpose:** Financial Terms & Conditions Confirmation Email — sent to client for plan confirmation before sharing payment link.

**When to use:** After receiving insurance company confirmation/approval on a lead's application, but before sending the payment link. Client must reply confirming the plan details before proceeding.

---

## How to Output This Email

**IMPORTANT:** When generating this email for a client, output as plain text (`.txt` file), NOT markdown. Email clients do not render markdown bullet points properly. Use:

- **Indent level 1 bullets:** `    •` (4 spaces before the bullet)
- **Indent level 2 bullets (sub-items):** `        -` (8 spaces before the dash)
- **Section headers:** ALL CAPS, no markdown heading syntax (e.g., `YOUR POLICY SUMMARY`)
- **Save as:** `Documents/[REF_ID]-[Name]/FTC_Email_Draft.txt` so the user can open and copy-paste directly
