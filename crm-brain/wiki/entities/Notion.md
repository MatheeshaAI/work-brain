# Notion

**Stub.** Workspace and database tool. In the early-2026 [[Work OS]] design it is the sales-pipeline surface and the approval screen: a database row per message or lead with properties such as Number, Text, Direction, Sent, NeedsFollowup, DraftMessage and Approved; a Notion integration token and shared database; webhooks or polling to trigger drafts ([[WhatsApp MVP and Notion Integration]], [[Work OS Full System Documentation]]). The AI OS guide lists "some Notion pages for client information" as part of Knowledge Management ([[AI OS 9 Components Guide for Insurance]]). Status: **documented, not built** ([[Work OS Master Index]]). This is separate from the vault itself, which is Obsidian ([[Obsidian]]).
