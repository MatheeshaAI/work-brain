# Insurancemarket.ae

The insurance brokerage the owner works for as a health-insurance business development manager. It is the **counterparty in every commission question**: it earns commission from insurers, applies the tiered scheme to the advisor's book, and issues the monthly statement that the salary is calculated from. Source: [[Commission Structure - Insurancemarket.ae]].

**The name appears in the vault in three roles**, and they are worth keeping apart:
1. **The employer.** It pays the base salary and the commission, processes the salary payment, and holds the tier table.
2. **The revenue counterparty.** It earns a **per-policy commission from each insurer** - 14-15% on individual and family plans, 2-7% on group and corporate. This is the `Comm%` column on the statement, and it is *not* the advisor's rate.
3. **The publisher of the statements.** The monthly CSV statement, and the ASR reports that the reconciliation work runs against, come from it.

## What it owes the advisor
- A **tier table** applied to the month's IM Revenue - 5% to 25% across six bands.
- A **guaranteed minimum commission** of AED 2,000 against a base salary of AED 8,000.
- A **monthly statement** on which the whole calculation rests.

## What the advisor still has to ask it
- **What "IM Revenue" means** - the Commission column total, or gross premium volume. The April 2026 numbers give different answers: 22,588.94 at 15% versus 158,098.55 at 25%. See [[Commission structure]] and [[Commission clarification queries]].
- **How deletions and cancellations** hit the monthly target, the allocated budget and reported revenue.
- **Which month a payment covers** - the salary received on 30 April, March or February.
- **The missing AED 2,000 MGI** on the 2026-05-06 salary payment. See [[Salary follow-up]].
- **Whether 5% VAT applies** on top of the personal commission as a service fee - an open question in the calculation note.
