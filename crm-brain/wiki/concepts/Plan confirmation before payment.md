# Plan confirmation before payment

Before a payment link goes out, the client confirms the plan and the terms in writing. The email that does it is the **FTC email** - Financial Terms & Conditions confirmation.

## The rule
"After receiving insurance company confirmation/approval on a lead's application, but before sending the payment link. **Client must reply confirming the plan details before proceeding.**"

So the order is fixed: insurer confirms the application -> FTC email goes out -> **client replies** -> payment link. The reply is not politeness; it is the step that makes the payment defensible.

## What the client is confirming
- **A policy summary**: insurer, chosen plan, network provider, co-insurance / deductibles, and who the invoice is raised to.
- **That the information provided is accurate** and that the client understands the policy terms, conditions and exclusions.
- **The document set**, with the warning that only the submitted documents will be considered once the client confirms, and that changes have to be raised before then.

## The terms the email carries
Network changes at the insurer's discretion; the medical declaration duty (all known or suspected conditions, symptoms and planned treatment - failure to disclose may affect coverage or eligibility); maternity coverage restricted to married females, at the insurer's discretion for undeclared pregnancies and for pregnancies arising within forty days of the application; and an optional outpatient-access block describing GP-referral-first and hospital access limited to emergency and approved in-patient treatment.

That last block is where the network question surfaces as a **terms** question rather than a provider question, which is why it connects to [[Customer objections]] and [[Explaining co-pay and plan terms]].

## Why plain text
The email is written to a `.txt` file in the lead's document folder, with indented bullets and ALL CAPS section headers, because it is pasted into a mail client. See [[Financial terms confirmation email SOP]].

## The known gap
The template's issuance turnaround is still the placeholder `xx working days`, and no price appears in the policy summary even though the email is titled Financial Terms & Conditions. The number has to be filled in per insurer before sending - and the one insurer whose timing is documented behaves differently again: see [[Takaful Emarat underwriting notes]].
