# Lead folder note template

Four layouts coexist in `Workspace/Leads` (see [[Workspace Leads folder overview]]).

| Layout | Used by | Sections |
|---|---|---|
| CRM-extract lead ("Lead Detail: <ref> - <name>") | 168 individual health leads | General Information, Customer Profile, Member Details/Information, Payment Details, Documents, Status Updates; optional Quote Details, Lead Status, Available Plans Extracted |
| Motor lead | 5 motor leads | General Information (with KYC and AML), Customer Profile, Vehicle Details, Insurance & Claims History, Quote & Payment, Documents, Notes |
| Renewal ("Renewal: <name> - <insurer> Policy <no>") | 20 renewals | Policy Details, Customer Profile, Status Updates, Renewal Quote, Documents, Payment |
| Company record | 1 group file | Company Details, Group Policy Transactions, Transaction Summary, Notes |

The Lead-Context file also gives a fifth, hand-written template with Contact Info first ([[Lead-Context files]]); it is not the one used in practice.

## Why it matters
The CRM-extract layout is generated by the extractor ([[IMCRM health detail page extraction]]), so field names mirror CRM labels (for example "Emirate of Visa", spelled two ways). Hand-added sections (Status Updates, Quote Details) carry the real progress. Names and chronology: [[Lead file naming]], [[Lead reference IDs]], [[Markdown-backed lead store]]. Quality problems: [[Lead data quality issues]].
