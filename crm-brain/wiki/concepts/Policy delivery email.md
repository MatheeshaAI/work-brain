# Policy delivery email

Email sent after policy booking to deliver documents and request a review. See [[SOP Policy Delivery Email Template]].

## Elements
- Subject: client name + "Policy Documents"
- Body: deliver attached documents, offer help, request Google review with link, thank/request to message after posting
- Attach: policy bond, e-card, insurance certificate, etc.

Cited: [[SOP Policy Delivery Email Template]], [[General underwriting rules]].
