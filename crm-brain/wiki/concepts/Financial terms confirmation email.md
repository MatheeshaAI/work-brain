# Financial terms confirmation email

Email sent after insurer confirms application and before payment link goes out. Client must reply to confirm. See [[SOP FTC Email Template]].

## Sections
- Opening (confirm accuracy, understand terms/exclusions)
- Policy summary (insurer, plan, network, co-insurance/deductible, invoice to)
- Application documents (forms/IDs/visa/sponsor)
- Key terms (network changes, medical declarations, maternity, outpatient access)
- Close (refer to TOB/policy wording, turnaround, request confirmation)

Output as plain text with specific formatting.
