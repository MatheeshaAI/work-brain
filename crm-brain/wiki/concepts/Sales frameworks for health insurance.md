# Sales frameworks for health insurance

With 100+ plan variations ([[Plan tiers and variables]]) the job is to narrow, not to list. The owner's chosen toolkit ([[Health Insurance Sales Frameworks]]):

- **Consultative discovery** first: three or four questions (conditions, preferred hospitals, visit frequency, premium versus cost-at-treatment, children and maternity, budget) reduce 50+ options to 3 to 5.
- **Three-option rule:** budget (Basic), balanced (Enhanced), international; inside Enhanced, entry/balanced/premium.
- **Value framing:** premium saved plus benefits minus expected co-pay equals real value, rather than quoting a price.
- **Education:** explain why co-pays exist and how TPA networks differ, with simple analogies.
- **SPIN** (situation, problem, implication, need-payoff) for objections; **Challenger** (teach, tailor, take control) for advanced cases.
- Process: discovery, present at most three, explain simply, handle objections, close ("Shall I process this today?").

Applied elsewhere in the wiki: [[Handling the price objection]], [[Customer objections]], [[Explaining co-pay and plan terms]], [[Customer categories]]. Caveat: the document is generic method, not tested against conversion data.
