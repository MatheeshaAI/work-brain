# Policy booking

Booking procedure after policy issuance. See [[SOP Booking Policy]].

## Steps
1. Open lead in CRM ([[IMCRM]]) once policy issued and all documents received.
2. Attach documents (member/sponsor IDs already attached at payment stage; add certificate, policy bond/schedule, e-cards, tax invoice, commission document).
3. Reply to insurer's AML/KYC check confirmation email (subject pattern: "client name - ref ID - AML & KYC Check").
4. Body: "all the documents are uploaded in the IMCRM, kindly book the policy and share the documents with the client".
