# Client privacy

Vault privacy rule for client data. Sources about clients (WhatsApp chats, leads, CRM exports) are summarized as patterns and numbers only. Do not copy client names, phone numbers, emails, lead IDs, payment details, ID/passport numbers, or health information into the wiki. Say which source holds the detail. Team members/referrers may be mentioned by role; individual names only when owner asks.
