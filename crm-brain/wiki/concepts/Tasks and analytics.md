# Tasks and analytics

Task tracker and sales analytics from [[Task & Sales Analytics System]].

## Task system
**Types:** New Lead, Renewal, Claims, Admin. **Priorities:** High/Medium/Low. **Status:** Pending/In Progress/Completed/Cancelled. Tracked in Task_Tracker.csv with due dates, follow-ups, time tracking.

## Daily workflow
Morning review today's high-priority tasks; update status/time/notes during day; evening mark complete/reschedule/plan tomorrow/update Lead_Tracker.

## Sales analytics
Tracks policies sold by type/company/client, premiums, tax, commission (avg ~15%). Snapshot shows 8 policies sold (6 health, 2 motor), commission AED 9,430. Companies: HAYAH, Takaful Emarat, Orient, Dubai Insurance, Sukoon.

## KPIs
**Sales:** 5-10 policies/week, 20-30% conversion, commission >=1000 AED/policy, response <24h. **Tasks:** >=90% completion, overdue <5, 8-10/day. **Pipeline:** 30-50 active, track velocity/drop-off/payment <3 days.

## Automation ideas
Auto-generate tasks by lead stage, auto-import sales reports, renewal reminders 30/60/90 days.
