# Negotiation stage stalls

Stage 5 of the CRM pipeline ("In Negotiation") is where the owner loses most leads. Not through a failed pitch but because he **forgets to follow up**; that is the stated reason the [[Leads Folder Sales Agent]] was started. Source: [[Sales Agent Requirements Conversation Log]] and [[Health Insurance Sales Agent Plan (Jan 2026)]].

## Why leads get stuck
1. Price looks too high; buyers expect unrealistic prices without knowing the market.
2. Buyers do not understand how health insurance works.
3. No response after the quotation.
4. Comparison shopping across competitors.
5. Window shopping, only collecting prices.
Also named: network gaps (wanted hospital groups absent from a plan), coverage confusion, trust ("hiding better options") and budget mismatch.

## Three buyer types
Quick buyers (quote, one or two questions, buy); slow buyers (many questions over time, then buy); comparison shoppers (find prices are about the same everywhere, come back). Matches the "comparison, then return" pattern in [[Handling the price objection]].

## Follow-up rules set for the system
- Follow-up kinds: no-answer call plus WhatsApp, post-quote, negotiation, documents.
- Follow up **48 hours** after a quote.
- Lost when the client says no or goes silent for X days; standard **3 to 5 follow-ups**, or **5 follow-ups counted from a proceed date** the client gives.
- When stuck on price the agent should find alternative plans, use honest urgency, value messaging and competitor analysis ([[Health Insurance Sales Frameworks]]).
- Next stages (9 to 16) run fast for clean cases and slowly with BMI or pre-existing conditions, which need reports and underwriting.

## Design intent versus fact
All of this is a plan. In practice later audits still show items ageing without follow-up ([[Sales System]], [[Sales operations issues]]), which is the same failure the plan targeted. Related: [[WhatsApp follow-up gaps]], [[Lead prioritization]], [[Insurance CRM pipeline]].
