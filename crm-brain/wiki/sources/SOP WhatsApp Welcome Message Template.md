# SOP WhatsApp Welcome Message Template

The one template behind [[Standard intro message]] and the captured email batch in [[Lead intro email batch]]. Concept page: [[Welcome message salutation rules]].

## Salutation rules
| CRM gender field | Salutation |
|---|---|
| Male, Male-Married, Male-Unmarried | Mr. |
| Female-Married | Mrs. |
| Female-Unmarried | Miss |
| Female with no marital status | Ms. |
| Under 18 (any) | first name only |

## Template (structure)
1. Greeting with first name and a wave emoji.
2. Introduction: the owner from InsuranceMarket.ae as the dedicated advisor for the health insurance enquiry.
3. "I've reviewed your profile and we have the perfect options for you."
4. The three tiers: **Basic Plan** (clinic coverage only, outpatient), **Basic Enhanced** (clinics plus direct access to specialists, outpatient), **Enhanced** (clinics plus hospitals, outpatient, plus international plans). See [[Plan price tiers]].
5. Two qualifying questions: a specific budget, or specific facilities (clinics, hospitals, international)?
6. Promise of tailored quotes once the preference is known; sign-off.

The system overview adds that all emojis are mandatory and must be copied character for character, and that referral leads get an adapted opening ([[Referral lead onboarding]]).

No prices appear in the welcome message; prices come with the quote ([[Handling the price objection]]).
