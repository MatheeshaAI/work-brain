# SOP Policy Delivery Email Template

Sent once a policy is booked and the documents can be shared. Concept page: [[Policy delivery email]].

- **Subject:** client name plus "Policy Documents".
- **Body:** greeting, "please refer to the attached policy documents for your [relation] insurance policy", an offer of further help, then a request for a quick Google review with a link, an optional mention of the advisor by name, and a request to message once posted so the advisor can thank the client.
- **Notes:** change the relation wording (your, your mother's, your family's); attach the policy bond, e-card, insurance certificate and any other relevant documents.

The review link in the file is a Google search link for the company's reviews; the system overview calls it a placeholder to be replaced with the actual review link, a small inconsistency ([[Health Insurance CRM system overview]]). The review ask links to [[Insurancemarket.ae]] reputation, and follows [[Policy booking]].
