# SOP Booking Policy

A one-paragraph SOP (dictated in speech style) for the last step of a sale. Full concept: [[Policy booking]].

## Steps as written
1. Once a policy is issued and all documents are received, open the lead in the CRM ([[IMCRM]]).
2. Attach all documents. Most of the member and sponsor identity documents (Emirates ID, passport, visa) are already attached at the payment-link stage.
3. Add the policy documents: health insurance certificate, policy bond / schedule, e-cards, tax invoice receipt and the commission document.
4. Send an email to the two booking addresses, copying the unit heads. It must be a **reply to the insurer's AML check confirmation email**, so the subject line follows the pattern "client name - ref ID - AML & KYC Check".
5. Body (fixed): greeting, "all the documents are uploaded in the IMCRM, kindly book the policy and share the documents with the client", thanks and regards.

## Notes
- The addresses are not named in the file. The system overview adds that the subagent drafts this email and the owner confirms before the status becomes Policy Booked ([[Health Insurance CRM system overview]]).
- The commission document ties booking to [[Commission calculation]].
