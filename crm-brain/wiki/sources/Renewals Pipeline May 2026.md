## Patterns in the ten
- Insurer mix: 6 [[Takaful Emarat]] (TE), 3 [[Dubai Insurance]] (DIC), 1 [[Oman Insurance]] (OI2 - corrected 2026-10-22; this page previously read `OI2` as [[Orient Insurance]]).
- Expiry dates run 8 May to 27 May; two or three per week, so one new contact a few days apart.
- Premium range AED 1,208.55 to 6,799.98. Five TE policies sit near AED 1,208-1,260 (the entry-level price band, see [[Insurance plan catalogue]]); the top three are DIC policies (6,799.98, 4,846.97, 2,923.65) and a TE policy at 5,490.45.
- All ten are existing CRM leads with "Allocated" status, so the renewal batch is cross-referenced to lead files.
- Checklist by week: first week, second week, mid-May, late May.
- One note: a DIC discount was declined by the insurer and the client notified, awaiting the client's decision (also in [[Workspace Task List 2026-05-22]]).

## Detail file for one renewal
A per-renewal file records policy details (provider, number, start, expiry, last year's premium), category (investor or partner), emirate of visa, client profile, renewal quote with the client's own budget range, follow-up action (prepare a Basic / Basic Enhanced comparison inside that budget; age-related underwriting applies - see [[Underwriting rules]]) and the CRM reference. The client is flagged as urgent because the policy expired on the day the file was written (20 May). Numbers and identifiers are not reproduced.

## Source lead record
The "_reference" file is a copy of the CRM lead created by a **renewal upload** (source "Renewal_upload", batch W21-2026, transaction type "Existing Customer's Renewal", status Allocated). It shows how the CRM models renewals: a lead per expiring policy, carrying previous policy number, expiry, premium, start date and previous advisor. A duplicate older lead from 2025 for the same client is kept for reference.
