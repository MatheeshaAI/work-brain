# Source: Todos

A short working task list: **four open items**, each a named client with their lead reference, their phone number, the subject of cover, and one line saying what is needed. **Only the themes are recorded here** - the source carries names, phone numbers, lead references and a medical detail, none of which are copied.

## The four tasks

| # | Subject of cover | Theme | What is blocking it |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Husband, wife and daughter | **E-cards** | The client is waiting for the e-cards of the insurance policy |
| 2 | Employee | **Submitting documents in the CRM** | Documents have been received; they still need submitting in IMCRM so the policy documents can be shared |
| 3 | Dependent child | **Underwriting refer-back** | A QA refer-back on the quote: the underwriter's approval is needed because a prior-year loading case had been applied; the desk is asking the client to confirm whether there was a pre-existing condition the previous year |
| 4 | Husband and wife | **Re-quotation** | The client is asking for quotations and the advisor needs to find the previous conversation first |
| | | | |

A short chat excerpt under task 3 shows the exchange pattern: the advisor opens in the evening, apologises for the delay, asks about last year's pre-existing condition, and asks which insurer covered the child the year before. The client's reply is that there was nothing. **No medical detail is reproduced here.**

## What the four tasks show
- **Two of four are stuck on document flow, not on selling.** E-cards and CRM submission are post-sale steps that the client is waiting on - see [[Document request handling]].
- **One is a quote-quality loop.** The quote was returned by QA for underwriter approval, the desk went back to the client for history, and the previous year's insurer matters because the prior year's underwriting set the terms. This is the same "existing cover changes the premium" point made in [[Call handling routine]].
- **One is a pure retrieval task.** The advisor's own note says to look up the last conversation to help with the quotation - the client had to prompt for it. See [[WhatsApp follow-up gaps]] and [[CRM hygiene]]: the record exists somewhere, and finding it is a task.
- **Every task names a lead reference and a phone number.** The list is the practical CRM: it is also the only place these clients are tracked as work in progress, which is why [[Open tasks]] treats it as a source of truth for the desk's own day.

## Contradiction to note
Task 4 asks the advisor to find the previous conversation for a client who is asking for quotations, while the same day's CRM export ([[IMCR Health Leads 07-Jun-2026]]) shows a lead at Quoted status with no quotation sent. Either the client is a repeat across an earlier cycle, or the status does not reflect what the customer has been told. Not resolved here.
