## Structure (sections in order)
Urgent (expired or expiring within 24 hours), Due this month not contacted, In progress, Quote sent / payment pending, Renewed, Did not renew / lapsed, Checklist (urgent today, this week). Status flow Due, Contacted, Quote Sent, Payment Pending, Renewed or Lapsed.

## Patterns
- Insurer mix of the 8: 3 with [[Takaful Emarat]] (TE), 4 with [[Dubai Insurance]] (DIC), and 1 already renewed with a different insurer (Live Insurance) as a combined family policy of AED 9,162.56.
- Three of the DIC policies belong to one family sharing one contact number; the file advises contacting once for all three (group contact rule, also in the renewal-scanner agent).
- Highest single premium: AED 34,013.70 (a TE policy flagged high value, due the day after the file's "today").
- Three DIC policies were already expired; a quote comparison was sent on 24 April and the file awaited confirmation.
- The update instruction: tell the agent "X contacted, sending quote of N with insurer" and it updates the renewal file, moves the row and updates the counts ([[Dual-update rule]]).
