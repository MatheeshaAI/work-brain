A report generated on 2026-06-08 that reviewed the health-insurance WhatsApp inbox for 3 May to 8 June 2026. **This page keeps only patterns and numbers.** The original lists client names, lead IDs, phone numbers and one client's health situation; none of that is copied here.

## Headline numbers (as of 2026-06-08)
- 136 unique conversations, deduplicated across snapshots, all active in the period. None found on 1-2 May.
- 84 (62%) were health-insurance lead conversations (lead IDs of the form HEA-xxxxxxxx); 52 (38%) were non-lead (team, insurers, personal).
- 16 conversations had unread messages.
- Volume by recent day: 8 on the report day, 29 the day before, 3 on the Saturday, 10 on the Friday, 9 on each of Wednesday and Thursday.

## Action items in the report (shape only)
- 3 urgent: a customer who paid more than a month ago and still has no policy documents; a customer who asked for a payment link with 2 unread messages; a quote that expired after the customer said they would return in June.
- Same-day follow-ups: an unsent draft about installment plans, a payment confirmation, a "can we now discuss?" reply, and a customer who said they would update soon.
- Cases needing the insurer: a policy issued under the wrong plan (see [[Dubai Care]]) and a customer who cannot make a second payment.
This is a **dated snapshot**; those items may have been resolved since.
